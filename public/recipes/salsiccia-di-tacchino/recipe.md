## Ingredients

- 1 medium onion, diced
- 2 medium garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 3 pounds ground turkey
- 4 ounces ground pork
- 7 ounces ground veal
- 1 scant cup (4 ounces) freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese
- 3 eggs, well beaten
- 2 tablespoons finely chopped flat-leaf parsley
- Grated zest of 1 1/2 lemons
- 1 small carrot, diced
- 50 pistachio nuts, shelled and toasted for 5 minutes in a 350°F oven
- 4 ounces best-quality mortadella, cut in 1/4-inch-thick slices and diced
- 2 teaspoons sea salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 3/4 teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Sauté the onion and garlic in the olive oil in a small sauté pan over low-medium heat until soft and pale, about 5 minutes, taking care not to burn the garlic. Set aside to cool.
3. Mix or grind together the ground turkey, pork, and veal and place in a large bowl. Stir in the Parmigiano-Reggiano and eggs. Add the onion and garlic mixture, parsley, lemon zest, carrot, pistachios, mortadella, salt, pepper, and nutmeg. Beat vigorously into the meat mixture with a wooden spoon.
4. Set the mixture on a sheet of aluminum foil at least 2 feet long. Pat the meat into a rectangle about 3 inches thick. Starting at the long end, use the foil to roll the meat into a long sausage-shaped log. Fold under both ends of the foil and press the ends and seam firmly to seal.
5. Place seam side down in a lightly oiled baking pan. Bake for 45 minutes, until the internal temperature is 140°F and the juices run clear.
6. Rest 10 to 15 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- The source gives this as the everyday version of the dish; for ceremonial occasions Nella Galletti made a boned, stuffed turkey galantine.
- Serve warm or at room temperature as a main dish. Thin slices also work as an antipasto with cornichons, black olives, and lightly pickled vegetables preserved under oil.
- It can be made a few hours ahead on the day it is served. Refrigerated, it keeps 2 to 3 days; bring it out at least 1 hour before serving so it can come to room temperature.
