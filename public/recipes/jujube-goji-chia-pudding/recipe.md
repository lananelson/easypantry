## Ingredients

- Chia seeds
- Almond milk
- Dried red jujubes, sliced
- Goji berries
- Dried osmanthus flowers
- Honey (optional)
- Yellow dragon fruit and kiwi, to top

## Instructions

1. Mix the chia seeds and almond milk with the jujubes, goji berries, and osmanthus. Add a drizzle of honey if you like.
2. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
3. Top with yellow dragon fruit and kiwi.
