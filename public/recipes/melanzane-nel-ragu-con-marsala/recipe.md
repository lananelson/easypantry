## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 globe eggplants, firm and glossy
- 1/2 red onion, diced
- 3/4 cup Salsa di Pomodoro Siciliana (page 430), with the addition of 3/4 cup chopped or torn fresh basil leaves
- 1/3 cup dry Marsala or red wine
- 2 cups water
- 1/2 teaspoon sea salt
- Pinch of sugar, optional

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil over high heat to 360°F in a deep, heavy pot such as a Dutch oven. Immerse the two whole eggplants in it. Reduce the heat to medium and cook for 15 minutes, turning them until entirely brown and distinctly soft.
2. Carefully remove the eggplants and set aside. Add the onion to the oil and sauté over medium heat until golden, 10 to 15 minutes.
3. Stir in 3/4 cup tomato sauce, reduce the heat to low, and cook, stirring frequently, until the sauce becomes very thick.
4. Add the Marsala and boil it down, stirring constantly.
5. Add the 2 cups water gradually, 1/2 cup at a time. The mixture will initially be very liquid but will reduce substantially in about 10 minutes. Continue until all the water has been added. Add salt and bring to a boil.
6. Return the eggplants to the pot and keep at a low boil for about 30 minutes, turning them from time to time, until the sauce is very thick.
7. Taste for sweetness and add a little sugar if desired. When the eggplants are done and the oil has risen to the top, move them carefully to a platter and drain for at least 1 hour.
8. Transfer the sauce to a bowl and refrigerate so the oil rises to the top. Pour off the oil. Warm the sauce when ready to serve and serve the eggplants at room temperature.

## Notes

- The source describes this as a Sicilian dish balancing tomato sweetness, soft eggplant, and Marsala.
- It can be made ahead to the draining/chilling stage early in the day.
- The Salsa di Pomodoro Siciliana used here is a separate recipe on page 430 of the same book; these photos do not include that recipe.
