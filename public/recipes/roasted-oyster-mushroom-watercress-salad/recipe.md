## Ingredients

- 1 small shallot, minced (about 2 tablespoons)
- 1 small clove garlic, minced (about 1/2 teaspoon)
- 2 teaspoons Dijon mustard
- 3 tablespoons white wine vinegar
- 1 tablespoon water
- 1 packed tablespoon roughly chopped fresh tarragon leaves
- 3/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- Vegetable or canola oil, for searing mushrooms
- 1 pound oyster mushrooms, firm central stems removed, caps separated, large caps torn by hand vertically into smaller pieces
- 4 ounces fresh watercress, cut or torn into smaller pieces if stalks are large
- 3 ounces Parmigiano-Reggiano or Grana Padano cheese, shaved with a vegetable peeler

## Instructions

1. Combine shallot, garlic, mustard, vinegar, water, and tarragon in a tall, narrow container. Using an immersion blender, blend until tarragon is finely chopped and incorporated. Alternatively, mince tarragon finely by hand and add to the base. Transfer to a large bowl. Whisking constantly, slowly drizzle in olive oil. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Set aside.
2. In a large cast iron skillet, heat 3 tablespoons vegetable oil over high heat until lightly smoking. Add just enough mushrooms to barely fill the skillet without crowding. Season with salt and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender and browned, about 5 minutes. Transfer to a baking sheet in an even layer to cool to room temperature. Repeat with remaining mushrooms, adding more oil before each batch.
3. In a large bowl, combine watercress with cooled mushrooms and cheese. Season lightly with salt and pepper. Toss with just enough vinaigrette to lightly coat, adding more as needed. Serve right away. Extra vinaigrette keeps refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 2 weeks.
