## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup sourdough starter
- 1 cup milk
- 2 1/2 cups flour (2 cups + 1/2 cup)
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- Cornmeal, for dusting

## Instructions

1. Mix the starter, milk, and 2 cups flour. Cover and leave overnight until bubbly.
2. The next day, add 1/2 cup flour, the sugar, salt, and baking soda. Mix, then knead on a floured counter into a smooth dough.
3. Roll out and cut into rounds.
4. Set the rounds on parchment dusted with cornmeal, dust the tops, cover with a towel, and let rest.
5. Cook on a griddle over medium-low heat until browned on both sides and cooked through. Cool on a rack.
