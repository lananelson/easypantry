## Ingredients

- 16 slices bacon (beef, turkey, or center-cut), each cut in half
- 8 eggs
- 500 g egg whites
- 300 g low-fat cottage cheese, blended smooth
- 5 g Parmesan
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Oil spray
- 8 bagel thins or low-carb bagels
- 8 slices cheddar

## Instructions

1. Bake the bacon in the oven until cooked.
2. Whisk the eggs and egg whites, then mix in the blended cottage cheese, Parmesan, and onion and garlic powder.
3. In a small pan over medium heat with a spray of oil, pour in just enough egg to cover the pan and cook a few minutes. Fold the edges into the middle and flip to make an egg pocket. Repeat to make 8.
4. Toast the bagels and fill each with an egg pocket, a slice of cheddar, and 4 bacon pieces.
5. Wrap in foil and freeze.

## Notes

- Use your smallest pan for the eggs; a bigger pan needs more egg to cover it.
- To reheat, move one to the fridge the night before, unwrap, wrap in a damp paper towel, and microwave 2–4 minutes. Don't microwave straight from frozen.
