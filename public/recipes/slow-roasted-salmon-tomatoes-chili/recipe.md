## Ingredients

- Plum tomatoes, halved
- Shallots
- Garlic
- Plenty of olive oil
- Salmon fillet
- Piment d'Espelette
- Red chili flakes
- Salt and pepper
- Red wine vinegar
- Fresh dill
- Rice, to serve

## Instructions

1. Roast the tomatoes, shallots, and garlic in a baking dish with plenty of olive oil. Lift them out to cool, leaving the oil.
2. Stir Espelette and chili flakes into the oil. Season the salmon with salt and pepper and coat it in the oil.
3. Slow-roast the salmon at 275°F until just cooked, buttery and flaky.
4. Meanwhile, chop the roasted tomatoes, shallots, and garlic and mix with red wine vinegar, salt, and pepper.
5. Break the salmon into large pieces, spoon over the tomato mixture and plenty of the chili oil, and top with dill. Serve with rice.
