## Ingredients

- 2 onions, roughly chopped
- 4 tomatoes, roughly chopped
- 1/2 inch ginger and 1 green chili, finely chopped
- 15 almonds and 8–10 cashews
- 2 dried red chilies
- 2 each black and green cardamom
- Bay leaf
- 1 tbsp coriander powder
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/2 cup water
- Honey
- Butter
- 1 tbsp Kashmiri red chili powder
- About 200 g paneer, diced
- Dried fenugreek leaves (kasoori methi), crushed
- Garam masala
- Cilantro, finely chopped

## Instructions

1. In a pressure cooker, combine the onions, tomatoes, ginger, green chili, almonds, cashews, dried chilies, cardamom, bay leaf, coriander powder, milk, water, honey, and a little butter. Mix well.
2. Cook on full flame for 1 whistle, then let the pressure release. Remove the whole spices and grind everything into a smooth, thick paste.
3. Melt butter in a pan, add the Kashmiri chili powder, then the paneer, and cook 2–3 minutes.
4. Add the masala paste and mix. Rinse the blender jar with a little water and add it.
5. Cover and cook 10 minutes.
6. Finish with crushed fenugreek leaves, a sprinkle of garam masala, and cilantro.
