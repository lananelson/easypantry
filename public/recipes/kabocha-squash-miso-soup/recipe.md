## Ingredients

- ⅓ small kabocha squash, cubed (about 200 g or ¼ medium kabocha squash)
- 1 dashi packet (traditional or vegan blend; use 2 for extra flavor)
- ½ onion, sliced
- 3 shiitake mushrooms, sliced (about ½ cup; other mushrooms such as maitake, shimeji, or king oyster also work)
- ½ lb silken tofu, cubed
- 2 tablespoons white miso (up to 4 tablespoons, to taste)
- 4 scallions, thinly sliced

## Instructions

1. In a medium pot, add ¼ cup of water, the kabocha, and a pinch of salt. Cover and cook over medium-low heat until the kabocha is tender, 5–10 minutes. If the pan looks dry, add a splash of water to prevent burning.
2. Add 3 cups of water and the dashi packet. Bring to a boil, then add the onion and mushrooms. Simmer until tender, about 3–5 minutes.
3. Add the tofu and return to a gentle simmer to warm through. Remove from heat.
4. Use a small sieve and chopsticks to stir miso into the soup. Taste and add more miso to your liking. Rewarm gently if necessary. Do not boil after adding miso.
5. Divide into serving bowls and top with scallions.

## Notes

- The source offers traditional or vegan dashi packets. Vegetable broth can replace dashi if unavailable.
- White miso is recommended, but regular miso also works; adjust to taste.
- The source suggests adding wakame, sesame seeds, sesame oil, chili crisp, or shichimi togarashi as garnish variations.
