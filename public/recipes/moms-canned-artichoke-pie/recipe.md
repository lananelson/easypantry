## Ingredients

- 1 small onion, diced
- Extra-virgin olive oil, for cooking
- 2 (14-ounce) cans artichokes in water, drained and excess moisture pressed out in a clean, lint-free dishtowel
- 7 large eggs
- 1 pound regular-milk mozzarella, diced
- 2 cups grated Parmesan
- Garlic powder, for dusting
- 2 store-bought disks refrigerated pie dough

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Sauté the onion in some olive oil until translucent, about 2 minutes.
3. Add the artichokes and sauté for another 2 minutes. Transfer to a bowl.
4. Add the eggs, mozzarella, Parmesan, and garlic powder to the bowl. Whip until the eggs are fluffy.
5. Lay 1 disk of pie dough in a 9-inch pie dish, then pour in the artichoke mixture.
6. Cover with the second disk of pie dough and seal the edges. Punch a few holes in the top.
7. Bake for exactly 1 hour.
8. Let cool for 30 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- Christian Petroni describes this as a special-occasion or holiday dish from his family, especially associated with Thanksgiving antipasti.
- It is good at room temperature, making it suitable for picnics.
- It can be made a day ahead and reheated for 5 to 10 minutes before serving.
