## Ingredients

- Wide noodles
- Gochujang
- Peanut butter
- Garlic
- 1 tsp chili flakes
- Sugar
- Soy sauce
- Rice vinegar
- Chicken powder
- Scallion
- Sesame seeds
- Neutral oil, heated

## Instructions

1. Cook the noodles and transfer them to a bowl.
2. Top with the gochujang, peanut butter, garlic, chili flakes, sugar, soy sauce, rice vinegar, chicken powder, scallion, and sesame seeds.
3. Pour hot oil over the top and mix well.
