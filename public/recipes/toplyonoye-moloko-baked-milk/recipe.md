## Ingredients

- 2 quarts milk (FairLife 2%, or good tasting whole milk)

## Instructions

1. Slow cooker: In a pot, slowly bring cold milk to simmer over medium heat, stirring. (This step prevents curdling of milk during the process of slow cooking.) Pour hot milk into the 3-quart insert of a crock pot. Cover with a lid and set on LOW. Do not disturb the milk or open the lid for another 8-12 hours.
2. Remove the skin from the top of the milk (either eat or discard it). Transfer the baked milk into a clean jar or bottle, let it cool to room temperature, and store refrigerated.
3. Pressure cooker (Instant Pot): Pour cold milk into the stainless steel inner pot (don't exceed the maximum fill line). Close the lid and set the steam release to sealed.
4. Select Meat/Stew and press Adjust to choose More for longer cooking. The pressure cycle takes about 2 hours. When finished, the cooker goes into Keep Warm, which continues for up to 10 hours. Bottle the milk right after the pressure cycle, or let it slowly cook for up to 10 more hours.
5. Remove the skin from the top of the milk, transfer to a clean jar or bottle, cool to room temperature, and refrigerate.

## Notes

- Any milk works, even half-and-half; whole or 2% is best. The more fat, the tastier.
- Slow-cooked baked milk is darker, thicker, sweeter, and more flavorful than pressure-cooked, since water evaporates.
- Use it anywhere you'd use milk: coffee or tea, oatmeal, crepe batter, pastry cream or custard. It can also be cultured into yogurt or soft cheese (ryazhenka is fermented baked milk).
- Best with a crock-pot with ceramic insert and heating elements around the sides, not only the bottom.
