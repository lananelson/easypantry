## Ingredients

- 2 lb kabocha squash (or butternut)
- 6 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper
- 2-inch piece ginger, minced
- 1 shallot, minced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/4 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp ground clove
- 1/4 tsp cayenne, plus more to garnish
- 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
- 4 cups water
- 4 tbsp maple syrup
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar (or lemon juice)
- 2 tbsp red miso
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 cup coconut milk (or heavy cream)
- Full-fat yogurt and fresh sage, to serve (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Seed the squash and cut into chunks. Toss with 3 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper and roast 40–45 minutes, until fork-tender.
2. Meanwhile, heat 3 tbsp olive oil in a pot over medium heat. Sauté the ginger and shallot about 4 minutes, then the garlic for 1 minute. Add the spices and toast 30 seconds.
3. Add the lentils and water. Bring to a boil, then simmer 30 minutes until soft.
4. Stir in the maple syrup, vinegar, miso, and tomato paste.
5. Remove the squash skin if you like, then purée the squash and lentils with the coconut milk. Adjust the salt and spices.
6. Serve with a dollop of yogurt, a pinch of cayenne, and sage.
