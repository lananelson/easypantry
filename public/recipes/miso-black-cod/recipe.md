## Ingredients

- Black cod fillets
- Sake, plus more for the marinade
- White miso
- Mirin
- Sugar
- Radish and green onion, to garnish

## Instructions

1. Wash the black cod with sake and pat dry.
2. Combine white miso, sake, mirin, and sugar. Marinate the fish in it for at least 2 days, ideally 3.
3. Wipe off most of the marinade and roast at 400°F for about 20 minutes.
4. Garnish with sliced radish and green onion.
