## Ingredients

- ¾ cup cooked white beans, like cannellini or navy (truffled cream sauce)
- 2 tablespoons water (truffled cream sauce)
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice (truffled cream sauce)
- 1 tablespoon nutritional yeast, optional (truffled cream sauce)
- 1 clove of garlic, peeled (truffled cream sauce)
- ¼ teaspoon white truffle oil (truffled cream sauce)
- sea salt & ground black pepper (truffled cream sauce)
- ½ bunch broccoli rabe, tough stems removed and chopped
- 1 tablespoon olive oil, plus extra
- 6 oz/185 grams shiitake mushrooms, stemmed and sliced
- ¾ teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 clove of garlic, minced
- 1 lb/454 grams pizza dough at room temperature
- flour of choice, for dusting
- 1 small shallot, thinly sliced
- 3-4 pickled pepperoncini peppers, drained and sliced
- ¼ cup sundried tomatoes, sliced
- 1 sprig oregano, leaves roughly chopped

## Instructions

1. Lay a pizza stone on the bottom rack of a cold oven, with enough clearance above to transfer the pizza. Preheat the oven to 500°F.
2. Truffled white sauce: in an upright blender combine the white beans, water, lemon juice, nutritional yeast, garlic, truffle oil, salt, and pepper. Blend on high until completely smooth — thick, but drips off a spoon, like a creamy salad dressing. Set aside.
3. Bring a large saucepan or deep skillet of water to a boil over high heat. Cook the chopped broccoli rabe for 1 minute. Drain and run cold water over it to stop the cooking. Dry with paper towels and set aside.
4. Wipe out the pan and set it back over medium-high heat. Add the tablespoon of olive oil and the sliced shiitakes. Sear for 1 minute. Stir, and add the paprika and garlic. Sear another full minute. Season with salt and pepper and stir — they should be glistening and soft. Scrape into a bowl and set aside.
5. Dust your work surface and a pizza peel with flour, or line the peel with parchment paper. Stretch the dough into a 12-inch circle of even thickness and transfer it to the peel. If using parchment, trim it to leave a ½ inch border around the dough.
6. Top the pizza with the truffled white sauce, broccoli rabe, shiitakes, shallots, pepperoncini, sundried tomatoes, and oregano.
7. Jiggle the peel to make sure the pizza will come loose. If it sticks, lift the edges and toss in a bit more flour.
8. Slide the pizza onto the hot stone with a few flicks of your wrist (or pull the parchment tab). Close the oven immediately.
9. Bake 7 minutes. Rotate the pizza with the peel and bake about 5 minutes more, until the crust is browning in spots and the mushrooms have some light char.
10. Drizzle lightly with olive oil before slicing and serving.

## Notes

- No pizza stone/peel: preheat the oven to 450°F with a half sheet pan inside for about 5 minutes. Carefully remove it, brush a drizzle of olive oil over it, and quickly transfer the stretched dough onto it (it should sizzle). Top and bake, checking after 15 minutes and cooking longer if needed.
