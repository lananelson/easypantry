## Ingredients

- 500 g / 1 lb ground lamb
- 1/2 large onion or 1 medium onion
- 1 egg
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 2 tsp sumac
- 2 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 3 tsp salt
- Handful chopped parsley
- 5 pita or Arab flatbreads
- Mozzarella (optional)
- 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
- 2 1/2 tbsp honey
- 3 tbsp canned chipotle in adobo
- 4 tbsp water
- 1/2 cup oil
- 1/4 tsp dried oregano
- Sour cream or yogurt, to taste

## Instructions

1. Grate the onion and add it to the lamb with the egg, garlic, cumin, cinnamon, coriander, sumac, 2 tsp black pepper, 1 1/2 tsp salt, and the parsley. Mix well.
2. Cook about 1 tbsp of the meat in a pan, taste, and adjust the seasoning if needed.
3. Cut the flatbreads in half and open up each half. Stuff in the meat until you're happy with the thickness. Add cheese if using and press the bread down gently.
4. Cook in a nonstick pan over medium heat for about 6 minutes per side, until the meat inside is fully cooked, then cook the open side with the meat facing down.
5. For the vinaigrette, blend the red wine vinegar, honey, chipotle in adobo, 1/2 tbsp salt, and water until smooth. With the blender running, slowly add the oil; you should have a bright orange sauce. Add 1/2 tsp black pepper and the oregano and blend again.
6. Mix the vinaigrette with as much sour cream or yogurt as you like and serve with the arayes.
