## Ingredients

- 1 can coconut milk
- About 2 tbsp fish sauce, to taste
- 1 lime, juiced, to taste
- Mushroom powder
- Lime powder (or 1 more lime)
- Lemongrass
- Makrut lime leaves
- Galangal
- Shallots
- Chilies (optional)
- Chicken thighs, or other protein such as fish balls, tofu, or pork
- King oyster mushrooms
- Green onions and cilantro, chopped

## Instructions

1. In a pot, combine the coconut milk, fish sauce, lime juice, mushroom powder, and lime powder. Adjust the lime for sourness.
2. Add the lemongrass, lime leaves, galangal, shallots, and chilies if using. Bring to a boil and taste.
3. Add the protein and let it boil without stirring, then stir and check that it's cooked.
4. Add the mushrooms and bring back to a boil, then turn off the heat.
5. Stir in the green onions and cilantro and serve.
