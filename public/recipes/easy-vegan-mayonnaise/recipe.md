## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons (45ml) liquid from 1 can of chickpeas, plus 12 whole chickpeas (about 1/2 ounce; 16 g)
- 2 teaspoons (10 ml) Dijon mustard
- 1 teaspoon (5 ml) fresh juice from 1 lemon
- 1 to 2 medium cloves garlic (5 to 10g), minced
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) vegetable oil
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. In a tall container just large enough to fit the head of an immersion blender, combine chickpea liquid, chickpeas, mustard, lemon juice, and desired amount of garlic. Blend at high speed until completely smooth, 1 to 2 minutes, stopping to scrape down sides as needed. Alternatively, blend in the jar of a standard 5-cup countertop blender.
2. With blender running at high speed, slowly add oil in a very thin stream, a few drops at a time, until a smooth, creamy emulsion forms, about 3 to 4 minutes total. If using a countertop blender, process for another 1 to 2 minutes on high speed to fully emulsify, if needed. Transfer to a bowl and season to taste with salt and pepper.

## Notes

- Special equipment: immersion blender or countertop blender.
- Mayonnaise may be slightly less smooth if made using a countertop blender, with some small pieces of garlic or chickpea remaining.
- Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to one week.
