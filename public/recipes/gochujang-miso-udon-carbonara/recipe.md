## Ingredients

- Udon noodles
- Pork belly or bacon, cut into pieces
- Garlic, minced
- Gochujang
- Miso
- Egg yolks, plus one to top
- Parmesan, grated, plus more to serve
- Black pepper
- Chives, to garnish

## Instructions

1. Render the pork belly in a pan until crisp.
2. Add the garlic, then the gochujang and miso, and stir with a splash of noodle water into a sauce.
3. Cook the udon and add it to the sauce.
4. Mix egg yolks with grated Parmesan and pepper, then stir into the noodles off the heat until creamy.
5. Top with a raw egg yolk, more Parmesan, and chives.
