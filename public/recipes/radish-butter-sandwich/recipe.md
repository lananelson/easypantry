## Ingredients

- Fresh baguette (or ciabatta or another rustic bread)
- Radishes, ideally with their greens
- Good salted butter, high butterfat
- Lemon
- Salt and pepper
- White anchovies (optional)

## Instructions

1. Slice the radishes paper-thin on a mandoline. Chop the radish greens.
2. Split the bread and spread butter generously on both sides.
3. Layer on plenty of radish slices.
4. Add a squeeze of lemon, salt, pepper, and the radish greens. Add white anchovies if you like.
5. Close and cut in half.
