## Ingredients

- 8 carrots
- 1 large white onion, thinly sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 4 sprigs thyme
- 1/2 cup white wine, stock, or water
- 1 tin butter beans, with their liquid
- 1/2 lemon, juiced, plus a squeeze for the garnish
- 1 tbsp cider vinegar
- Olive oil
- Salt
- 2 tbsp flaked almonds
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- Parsley, finely chopped
- Pinch of sugar

## Instructions

1. Peel the carrots and steam for 7–10 minutes. Shock in cold salted water.
2. Cut the carrots lengthways and fry over medium heat in a generous amount of olive oil, ideally pressed under a weight such as a heavy pan.
3. For the purée, sauté the thyme, onion, and garlic in a generous amount of olive oil over medium heat until soft and lightly browned. Add the wine and simmer about 5 minutes.
4. Transfer to a blender with the butter beans and their liquid, the lemon juice, and the vinegar. Blend 2 minutes on medium, then 2 minutes on high, adding a splash of water if needed. Season with salt.
5. In the carrot pan, toast the almonds and jalapeño for about a minute. Add the parsley, a squeeze of lemon, a little olive oil, and a pinch of salt and sugar.
6. Spread the purée on a plate, lay the carrots on top, and spoon over the almond garnish.
