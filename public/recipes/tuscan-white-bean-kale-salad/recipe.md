## Ingredients

- 4 cups lacinato kale, destemmed and chopped
- 1 can artichokes, drained and chopped
- 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 can white beans, drained and rinsed
- 7 oz jar sun-dried tomatoes, drained and chopped
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
- 1 bunch fresh parsley
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced, plus a squeeze for the kale
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 1/3 cup raw walnuts
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

## Instructions

1. For the dressing, pulse the parsley, garlic, walnuts, and lemon zest in a food processor until finely chopped. Add the lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper and blend, leaving some texture. Thin with up to 2 tbsp water.
2. Massage the kale with a squeeze of lemon until it softens.
3. Add the artichokes, chickpeas, white beans, sun-dried tomatoes, avocado, and Parmesan, and fold in the dressing.
