## Ingredients

- 4 russet potatoes
- 6 tbsp salted butter (4 for the mash, 2 for the sauce)
- 3 egg yolks
- Salt and white pepper
- 1 large leek
- 2 shallots
- 1/2 bulb fennel
- 1 tbsp oil
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 cups milk
- 1 cup cream
- 1 lb fish of your choice, cut into chunks
- Zest of 1/2 lemon
- 1 tbsp chopped dill
- 1 tbsp chopped parsley
- 1/2 tbsp chopped tarragon
- 1/4 tsp grated nutmeg

## Instructions

1. Boil and mash the potatoes with 4 tbsp butter, the egg yolks, salt, and white pepper.
2. Slice the leek, shallots, and fennel and sauté in 2 tbsp butter and the oil until the leeks are tender.
3. Add the flour and cook 2 minutes. Slowly whisk in the milk, then the cream, and cook about 3 minutes until thickened.
4. Stir in the fish, lemon zest, herbs, nutmeg, salt, and white pepper, then turn off the heat.
5. Spread in a baking dish and top with the mashed potato.
6. Bake at 375°F for about 35 minutes, until golden brown.
