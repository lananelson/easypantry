## Ingredients

- Beef chuck or stewing lamb, cubed
- Onion, diced
- Salt and pepper
- Turmeric
- Tomato paste
- Kidney beans
- Dried limes (limoo omani)
- Water
- Scallions, chopped
- Cilantro, chopped
- Parsley, chopped
- Spinach, chopped
- Dried fenugreek
- Oil
- Rice and tahdig, to serve

## Instructions

1. Sauté the onion and meat with salt, pepper, turmeric, and tomato paste over medium-high heat until well browned.
2. Add the kidney beans and dried limes, cover with water, and simmer covered for 1 hour.
3. Meanwhile, fry the chopped herbs and fenugreek in oil for 15–20 minutes, until most of the water has cooked off.
4. Stir the herbs into the stew and simmer about 4 more hours.
5. Season with salt and serve over rice.
