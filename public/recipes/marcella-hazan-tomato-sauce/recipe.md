## Ingredients

- 2 pounds fresh, ripe tomatoes (plum, or other truly fruity varieties), prepared as below, or 2 cups canned imported Italian tomatoes, cut up, with their juice
- 5 tablespoons butter
- 1 medium onion, peeled and cut in half
- Salt to taste
- Freshly grated parmigiano-reggiano, for the table

## Instructions

1. If using fresh tomatoes, prepare them one of three ways. Blanching: plunge the tomatoes in boiling water for a minute or less, drain, skin them when cool enough to handle, and cut into coarse pieces. Freezing (David Tanis, via The Kitchn): freeze tomatoes on a baking sheet until hard, thaw on the counter or under running water, then skin and cut into coarse pieces. Food mill: wash and halve the tomatoes lengthwise, cook covered over medium heat for 10 minutes, then puree through a food mill fitted with the largest disk.
2. Put the tomatoes in a saucepan, add the butter, onion, and salt, and cook uncovered at a very slow, but steady simmer for about 45 minutes, or until it is thickened to your liking and the fat floats free from the tomato.
3. Stir from time to time, mashing up any large pieces of tomato with the back of a wooden spoon.
4. Taste and correct for salt. Before tossing with pasta, you may remove the onion (as Hazan recommended) and save for another use, but many leave it in. Serve with freshly grated parmigiano-reggiano.
