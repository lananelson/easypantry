## Ingredients

- Eggs
- Oil
- Salt and white pepper
- Scallions
- Tomatoes, cut into chunks
- Soy sauce
- Oyster sauce
- Ketchup
- Sugar
- Water
- Cornstarch slurry
- Rice or noodles, to serve

## Instructions

1. Scramble the eggs in a hot wok with oil, seasoning with salt and white pepper. Set aside.
2. Sauté the scallions until they start to brown, then add the tomatoes.
3. Season with soy sauce, oyster sauce, ketchup, and sugar. Add water and simmer a few minutes.
4. Pour in the cornstarch slurry and let it thicken.
5. Fold in the scrambled eggs and serve with rice or noodles.
