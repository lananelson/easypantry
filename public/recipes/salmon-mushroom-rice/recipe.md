## Ingredients

- 2 cups rice
- 1.75 cups water
- 1 tbsp sake
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp dashi powder
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- Mushrooms, such as shimeji and oyster
- Salmon fillet
- Shichimi togarashi
- Shiso or herbs, green onion, and pickled red ginger, to serve

## Instructions

1. In the Instant Pot, combine the rice, water, sake, mirin, soy sauce, dashi powder, and ginger.
2. Top with the mushrooms and the salmon.
3. Pressure cook on the rice setting.
4. Break up the salmon and mix it through the rice.
5. Serve topped with shichimi, herbs, green onion, and pickled ginger.
