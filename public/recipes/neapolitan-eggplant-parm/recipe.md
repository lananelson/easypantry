## Ingredients

- 3 large eggplants
- 1 cup kosher salt, for sprinkling
- Extra-virgin olive oil, for frying, plus more for drizzling
- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons freshly ground pepper
- 1 quart [8-hour Marinara](../8-hour-marinara/recipe.md)
- 1 chunk Parmigiano-Reggiano (the size of a large orange)
- 1 basil plant from the supermarket
- Special equipment: a deep-fry thermometer

## Instructions

1. Peel the eggplants and cut them into 1/4-inch slices. Salt and let sit for 20 minutes, then pat dry.
2. In a heavy or cast-iron pan, add enough olive oil for a shallow fry, about 1/2 inch. Heat the oil to 350°F.
3. Add the flour to a medium bowl and season with the pepper. Lightly flour the eggplant and fry until light golden brown.
4. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
5. In an 8-inch casserole dish or brownie pan, add a layer of marinara, about 1/4 inch.
6. Arrange eggplant slices to cover the sauce. Top with more marinara, a generous dusting of Parmigiano-Reggiano, and torn basil leaves.
7. Repeat the layers — eggplant, sauce, cheese, basil — until the dish is full. Leave the basil off the top layer (it will burn); instead, drizzle generously with olive oil.
8. Bake for 30 minutes, until browned and bubbly.
9. Let sit for 30 to 60 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- Neapolitan-style eggplant Parmigiana isn't necessarily breaded: the eggplant is fried, then layered with tomato sauce and Parmigiano-Reggiano until the pan is full, baked, and cut into squares.
- Traditionally you don't cut all the way through the square with your fork; you peel the layers of eggplant off the top and work your way down.
