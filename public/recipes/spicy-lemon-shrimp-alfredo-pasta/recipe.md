## Ingredients

- Shell-on shrimp (peels and tails saved for stock)
- Leftover onion scraps
- Garlic powder
- Paprika
- Cayenne
- Avocado oil
- Salt and pepper
- Olive oil
- Red pepper flakes
- Garlic, minced
- Tomato paste
- Butter
- Flour
- Half-and-half
- Lemon juice
- Capers
- Spinach
- Linguine or spaghetti
- Pecorino Romano, grated, lots
- Truffle oil, to finish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Peel the shrimp. Simmer the peels and tails with leftover onion scraps in boiling water for about 10 minutes to make a shrimp stock.
2. Season the shrimp with garlic powder, paprika, cayenne, avocado oil, salt, and pepper. Cook them, then transfer to a plate.
3. In the same pan, sauté red pepper flakes, minced garlic, and tomato paste in olive oil. Add butter and flour and cook off the raw flour taste.
4. Pour in the half-and-half, lemon juice, capers, and shrimp stock. Add spinach and let it wilt. Adjust salt and pepper to taste.
5. Add the shrimp back to the sauce along with the cooked pasta.
6. Top with a generous amount of pecorino Romano and toss until well combined. Finish with a drizzle of truffle oil if you like.
