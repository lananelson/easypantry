## Ingredients

- 2 cups red lentils
- 1 potato
- 1 carrot
- 1 onion
- 4 cloves garlic
- 2 lemons
- Parsley, to taste
- 1 loaf Arabic bread (khubz arabi)
- Cumin, to taste
- Salt, to taste
- Black pepper, to taste
- 1 liter water

## Instructions

1. Rinse the red lentils thoroughly.
2. Peel the potato and dice it small. Chop the carrot into small pieces, roughly dice the onion, and mince the garlic. Slice the lemons, finely chop the parsley, and cut the Arabic bread into thin strips.
3. In a pan over medium heat, sauté the onion, potato, carrot, and garlic with cumin, salt, and pepper until the vegetables are deeply roasted.
4. Add the lentils and the water. Bring to a boil, then simmer for about 20 minutes, until the lentils are tender.
5. Meanwhile, fry the bread strips in a separate pan until golden brown.
6. Transfer the soup to a blender and blend until smooth and velvety.
7. Squeeze the lemon juice into the soup and stir in the chopped parsley.
8. Ladle into bowls and garnish with the fried bread strips and more parsley.
