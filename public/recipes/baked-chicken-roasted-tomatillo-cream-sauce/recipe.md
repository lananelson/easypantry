## Ingredients

- 1/2 pound tomatillos, husked and rinsed
- 2 large cloves garlic, peeled
- 2 serrano chiles, stemmed (or to taste)
- 1/3 cup loosely packed cilantro, plus more for garnish
- salt to taste
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, or 1 whole chicken, cut into pieces

## Instructions

1. On a small baking sheet, spread out the tomatillos, garlic cloves, and serrano chiles. Roast under a hot broiler until charred and soft, turning once during cooking, about 5 minutes per side. Set the oven to 400 degrees.
2. Once the vegetables have cooled slightly, blend with the cilantro and just enough water to bring it together (1/4 cup or so). Taste and season with salt.
3. In a medium bowl, whisk together the tomatillo salsa and the cream. Put the chicken in a baking dish that holds everything snugly in one layer and cover with the tomatillo-cream sauce.
4. Bake the chicken until just cooked through, 15-30 minutes depending on the size of the pieces.

## Notes

- The roasted tomatillo salsa (steps 1-2) is great on its own with tortilla chips.
