## Ingredients

- Extra-firm tofu
- Oil, for frying
- Palm sugar
- Galangal
- Garlic
- Black pepper
- Green onions, white parts
- Shallots
- Red onion
- Mushroom seasoning
- Vegan fish sauce
- Lemon juice
- Salt
- Chilies
- Cilantro
- Mint
- [Khao khua](../khao-khua-toasted-rice-powder/recipe.md) (toasted rice powder)
- Sticky rice, to serve

## Instructions

1. Tear the tofu into rough pieces with your hands, so the ragged edges crisp up, and fry until golden and crunchy.
2. In a mortar, pound the palm sugar, galangal, garlic, pepper, green onion whites, shallots, and red onion.
3. Add the mushroom seasoning, vegan fish sauce, lemon juice, and salt to make the dressing base.
4. Add the tofu and chilies and pound lightly to coat.
5. Transfer to a bowl and toss with cilantro, mint, and plenty of khao khua.
