## Ingredients

- 1 lb chicken thighs
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 1 tsp oil, plus more for the wok
- 4 tbsp soy sauce (1 for the marinade, 3 for the sauce)
- 4 cloves garlic
- 2–3 bird's eye chilies
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
- 1 tbsp palm sugar or brown sugar
- 3 stalks gai lan (or broccolini)
- 1 big handful Thai basil
- 8 oz fresh wide rice noodles
- Green onion, to garnish

## Instructions

1. Slice the chicken into thin strips and mix with the baking soda, cornstarch, 1 tsp oil, and 1 tbsp soy sauce.
2. Bruise the garlic and chilies in a mortar and pestle (or chop). Add a teaspoon to the chicken and set the rest aside.
3. Separate the gai lan stems from the leaves and chop the stems. Pick the basil leaves. Mix 3 tbsp soy sauce, the oyster sauce, dark soy, fish sauce, Shaoxing wine, and sugar.
4. In an oiled wok, lay the chicken flat to sear. When the edges are cooked, stir and cook a minute more, then remove.
5. Meanwhile, prepare the rice noodles according to the package.
6. Fry the garlic and chilies in the wok, add the gai lan stems, and cook 1 minute.
7. Add the noodles and sauce and let the noodles caramelize.
8. Add the gai lan leaves, basil, and chicken and toss until the leaves wilt. Garnish with green onion and more basil.
