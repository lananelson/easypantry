## Ingredients

- 1 bunch garlic chives
- 4 large eggs
- 1 1/2 teaspoons soy sauce
- freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 1 teaspoon vegetable oil
- 2 tablespoons vegetable or peanut oil

## Instructions

1. Wash and dry the garlic chives. Remove any hard ends and chop into small pieces.
2. Lightly beat the eggs. Add the soy sauce (or salt), 1 teaspoon vegetable oil and pepper.
3. Heat a frying pan or wok on a medium high heat and add the remaining oil. When the oil is hot, add the chives.
4. Stir-fry briefly, then add the beaten egg mixture.
5. Reduce the heat to medium and gently scramble the eggs until they reach the consistency that you like.
6. Serve over rice.

## Notes

- Add in some scallions for flavor as well.
