## Ingredients

- 4 bulbs roasted garlic
- 1 large baguette
- 4 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 pound brussels sprouts, sliced or shredded
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon pepper
- pinch of nutmeg
- 2 tablespoons freshly grated parmesan cheese
- shaved parmesan cheese for topping

## Instructions

1. Roast the garlic ahead of time if needed (a day or two is fine). Let it cool, squeeze the cloves out, removing any of the paper, and mash with a fork until creamy and spreadable.
2. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F. Slice baguette into 1/2-inch rounds and place on a baking sheet. Drizzle with 2 tablespoons of olive oil. Bake for 8-12 minutes, rotating the pan halfway through.
3. While the bread is toasting, heat a large skillet over medium heat. Add remaining olive oil, then toss in brussels sprouts, salt, pepper and nutmeg, tossing well to coat. Cook for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until warm, wilted, slightly crispy and a little golden. Stir in grated parmesan, mixing well to coat, and turn off heat.
4. Spread a bit of mashed roasted garlic onto each piece of crostini. Top with a spoonful of sprouts, a shaving of fresh parmesan and some cracked black pepper.
