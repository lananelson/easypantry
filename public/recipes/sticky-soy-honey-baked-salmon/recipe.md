## Ingredients

- Salmon fillet
- 100 ml mirin
- 100 ml soy sauce
- 50 ml dark soy sauce
- 50 ml honey
- 3 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp sesame oil
- 10 garlic cloves
- 2 thumb-sized pieces ginger
- 5 spring onions, plus more to garnish
- 4 cloves
- 1 star anise
- 150 ml water
- 2 lemons, sliced
- Sesame seeds, to garnish

## Instructions

1. Put the mirin, soy sauce, dark soy sauce, honey, brown sugar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, spring onions, cloves, star anise, and water in a pot. Cook over high heat until reduced to a thick sauce, about 10 minutes.
2. Lay the lemon slices in a baking dish, set the salmon on top, and pour the sauce over it.
3. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 25–30 minutes, basting with the sauce throughout.
4. Garnish with spring onion and sesame seeds.

## My Notes

- Good to make with the large Costco salmon fillet.
