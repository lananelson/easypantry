## Ingredients

- 6 super-colossal shrimp or 18 to 24 jumbo shrimp
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 4 large eggs
- 1 cup grated pecorino (preferably Locatelli)
- 8 to 12 grinds freshly ground black pepper
- 3 cups [seasoned Italian breadcrumbs](../seasoned-italian-breadcrumbs/recipe.md) or [homemade SIBS](../homemade-sibs/recipe.md)
- 1/2 bunch fresh flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped
- 1 1/2 cups grated Parmesan
- Extra-virgin olive oil, for frying
- 1 pint [8-Hour Marinara Sauce](../8-hour-marinara/recipe.md)
- 1 (8-ounce) ball fresh mozzarella
- 2 tablespoons dried Sicilian oregano
- 1 loaf Italian bread, for serving
- [Garlic Butter](../garlic-butter/recipe.md), for serving

## Instructions

1. Butterfly the shrimp. Place them between two slices of plastic wrap and gently pound with a mallet or the bottom of a small sauté pan until about 1/4 inch thick and resembling thin chicken cutlets. Set aside.
2. Prepare three shallow bowls for breading. Put the flour in the first bowl.
3. In the second bowl, combine the eggs, pecorino, and black pepper. Beat with a fork until completely combined.
4. In the third bowl, combine the seasoned breadcrumbs with the chopped parsley and 1 cup of the Parmesan.
5. Bread the shrimp in this order: flour, egg mixture, breadcrumb mixture.
6. Pour enough olive oil into a heavy pan with sloped sides, or a cast-iron pan, so that the bottom halves of the shrimp will be submerged. Heat the oil to 350°F.
7. Fry the shrimp until golden brown on both sides, about 3 minutes per side. Transfer to a rack or paper towels.
8. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
9. Spoon enough marinara onto a small sheet tray to lightly cover the bottom.
10. Arrange the fried shrimp on the tray. Spoon a few more tablespoons of marinara over each shrimp, leaving some of the crispy edges exposed.
11. Slice the mozzarella into 1/2-inch-thick slices and place a slice on each shrimp. Sprinkle with the remaining 1/2 cup Parmesan and the oregano. Drizzle with olive oil.
12. Bake until the mozzarella is melted and the Parmesan forms a textured shell, about 7 minutes.
13. Serve as a sandwich on garlic-buttered toasted bread, or as the center of the plate with vegetables and roasted potatoes or spaghetti.

## Notes

- Christian Petroni describes Shrimp Parm as a deliberately over-the-top Italian-American dish: butterflied, pounded shrimp are treated like chicken cutlets, fried, then topped with marinara and cheese.
- When saucing the fried shrimp, leave some edges uncovered so they stay crisp after baking.
- Special equipment: deep-fry thermometer.
