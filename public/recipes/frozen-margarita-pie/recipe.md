## Ingredients

- 1 cup finely crushed pretzels (crust)
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted (crust)
- 2 Tbs sugar (crust)
- Canola oil cooking spray (crust)
- 6 Tbs fresh squeezed lime juice, about 5 limes
- freshly grated zest of lime (used all of the limes)
- 2 Tbs fresh squeezed orange juice (1 orange)
- 1 can Eagle Brand sweetened condensed milk
- 1 pint heavy whipping cream
- tequila (1 nip)
- 1 Tbs Cointreau or Triple Sec
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Lightly oil a 9-inch pie pan with canola oil cooking spray.
2. To prepare the crust, grind the pretzels to a fine meal in a food processor. Add the butter and sugar and pulse to combine. Press into the prepared pie pan to form the crust. Bake for 5-7 minutes, until the edges turn slightly golden. Cool before filling (I placed it in the freezer while making the filling).
3. In a medium bowl, stir together condensed milk, zest, juices, tequila, liqueur, and salt. Let stand for 5 minutes to thicken.
4. Whip cream to a stiff consistency. Gently fold all the other mixed ingredients into the whipped cream. Pour into the pie crust and smooth out softly.
5. Freeze at least 2 hours. Best served frozen.
