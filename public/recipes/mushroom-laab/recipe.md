## Ingredients

- Oyster mushrooms
- Galangal
- Garlic
- Black pepper
- Palm sugar
- Green onions
- Shallots
- Mushroom seasoning
- Lime juice
- Vegan fish sauce
- [Khao khua](../khao-khua-toasted-rice-powder/recipe.md) (toasted rice powder)
- Cilantro
- Mint
- Lettuce leaves and chilies, to serve

## Instructions

1. In a mortar, pound the galangal, garlic, pepper, and palm sugar. Add the green onions, shallots, mushroom seasoning, lime juice, and vegan fish sauce to make the laab dressing.
2. Sear the oyster mushrooms until browned, pressing them down for a meatier texture.
3. Toss the mushrooms with the dressing, plenty of khao khua, cilantro, and mint.
4. Serve with lettuce leaves and chilies.
