## Ingredients

- 2 boxes no-boil lasagna noodles
- 6 cups marinara sauce
- 2 cans great northern or cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 2 oz. fresh basil leaves (no stems)
- 1 cup hemp seeds (or raw cashews)
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1/4 cup miso paste
- 1/4 cup nutritional yeast
- 1/4 lemon juice (as written in the original; probably 1/4 cup)
- 1/8 tsp red pepper flakes
- 2 lbs. frozen spinach, thawed, drained and squeezed
- 2 lbs. mushrooms, sliced
- 1/4 cup low-sodium tamari or soy sauce
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 1 cup raw almonds or cashews (faux parmesan)
- 1/2 cup nutritional yeast (faux parmesan)
- 1 tbsp salt-free Italian seasoning (faux parmesan)

## Instructions

1. In a food processor, add the beans, basil, hemp seeds, 3 cloves of garlic, miso paste, nutritional yeast, lemon juice and pepper flakes. Puree until smooth.
2. Add the spinach and puree again. Set aside.
3. In a large sauté pan, sauté the onions in 2 tbsp of water until translucent, about 8 minutes, adding more water when needed.
4. Add 1 clove of garlic, the mushrooms, and the tamari and sauté until golden brown. Cook until mushrooms look glazed and no liquid is left in the pan.
5. Faux parmesan: combine almonds or cashews, nutritional yeast, and Italian seasoning in a food processor until a powdery texture forms. Keeps in the refrigerator.
6. Pour 3 cups of marinara sauce into a 9 x 13 lasagna pan.
7. Place 1 layer of no-boil lasagna noodles on top of the sauce.
8. Cover the noodles with about 1/3 of the spinach mixture. Then add a layer using about 1/2 of the mushroom mixture.
9. Repeat: 1 layer of noodles, 1/3 spinach mixture, 1/2 mushroom mixture.
10. Place 1 more layer of noodles. Layer the remaining spinach mixture on top.
11. Smother evenly with 3 cups of marinara sauce, making sure all the noodles are completely covered.
12. Sprinkle with faux parmesan.
13. Bake uncovered in a pre-heated 375°F oven for 1 hour.
14. Let stand 10 minutes before slicing.

## Notes

- Gluten-free: use gluten-free lasagna or rice noodles, or bake thin eggplant slices for 20 minutes and use in place of noodles.
- Extra firm tofu can replace the beans; frozen kale can replace the spinach; pine nuts can replace the hemp seeds or cashews.
- Sliced black olives can be sprinkled on top before baking.
- Refrigerated leftovers slice more cleanly than hot lasagna.
- No-oil recipe.
