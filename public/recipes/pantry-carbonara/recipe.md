## Ingredients

- Pasta
- Hard cheese, grated
- Egg yolks
- Shallots
- Garlic
- Fresh parsley
- Olive oil
- Freshly cracked black pepper
- Chili flakes
- Caramelized onion salt (optional)

## Instructions

1. Mix the grated cheese and egg yolks together. Chop the garlic, shallots, and parsley.
2. Cook the pasta until al dente, saving some pasta water.
3. Sauté the shallots in hot olive oil for a couple of minutes.
4. Add the garlic, black pepper, chili flakes, and the onion salt if using. Cook a few more minutes, careful not to burn the garlic and spices.
5. Add the pasta and a good splash of pasta water.
6. Temper the egg mixture with more pasta water so it doesn't curdle, then pour it over the pasta and stir vigorously to emulsify. The sauce should be silky, thick, and glossy. Finish with the parsley.
