## Ingredients

- 1 large slice sourdough
- 1 can sardines (Moroccan preferred)
- 2 tbsp harissa
- 1 tbsp chopped cilantro
- Juice of ½ lemon
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- Cumin, salt, and pepper, to taste
- 1 Hass avocado
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- ½ tbsp chopped cilantro
- ½ tsp za'atar
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp cumin
- Salt, to taste
- Za'atar, lemon zest, chopped cilantro, and pomegranate molasses, to garnish

## Instructions

1. Mash the sardines until flaky, then mix in the harissa, 1 tbsp cilantro, juice of ½ lemon, garlic, cumin, salt, and pepper. Set aside.
2. Mash the avocado with the za'atar, ½ tbsp cilantro, garlic powder, cumin, and salt, then mix in about 1 tbsp lemon juice.
3. Toast the bread extra well so it holds up to the toppings.
4. Spread the avocado on the toast and top with the sardines.
5. Garnish with pomegranate molasses, cilantro, lemon zest, and za'atar.

## Notes

- Chickpeas can be used instead of sardines.
- Inspired by the sardine toast the author ate growing up in Morocco; works as a quick snack or a full meal.
