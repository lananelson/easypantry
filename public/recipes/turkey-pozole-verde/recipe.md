## Ingredients

- 1 turkey carcass
- 2 white onions, quartered with skins on
- 8 garlic cloves
- 4 celery stalks, chopped
- 2 large carrots, scrubbed and chopped
- 1 large bunch hardy herbs, such as thyme, oregano, or marjoram
- 4 bay leaves
- Kosher salt
- 2 cups dried white or golden hominy, refrigerated in water overnight
- 2 tbsp dried oregano, plus extra to serve
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- ½ white onion, roughly chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, smashed
- 1 cup raw pepitas
- 4 allspice berries
- 2 whole cloves
- ½ tsp cumin seeds
- 1 lb tomatillos, husked, rinsed, and roughly chopped
- 3–5 serranos, optionally seeded
- 1 cup radish tops, arugula, spinach, or other greens
- Kosher salt
- 8 radishes, sliced thin
- 2 cups shredded iceberg or green cabbage (optional)
- 1 cup crushed chicharrones (optional)
- ¼ cup crushed pequin chiles or red pepper flakes
- Lime wedges

## Instructions

1. Cover the carcass, quartered onions, garlic, celery, carrots, herb bunch, and bay leaves with 4 quarts water, adding more if needed to submerge the turkey. Bring to a boil, skim the surface, and simmer for 3 hours.
2. Lift out the carcass and strain the broth. Return the broth to low heat, salt to taste, and set aside 2 cups for the green sauce.
3. Drain the hominy and trim off the tips of the kernels. Add the hominy and 4 cups water to the main pot of broth. Cover and simmer for 2–3 hours, until the kernels are tender. Take off the heat and stir in the dried oregano. Pick and shred the meat from the carcass while the hominy cooks.
4. Soften the chopped onion and smashed garlic in the oil. Stir in the pepitas and cook until lightly golden. Add the allspice, cloves, and cumin and cook until fragrant. Blend this mixture with the tomatillos, serranos, and the reserved broth until smooth.
5. Simmer the green sauce for about 10 minutes to thicken slightly. Blend 1 cup of the sauce with the greens until smooth, then add both portions of sauce to the hominy. Adjust the salt, stir in the turkey, and serve with the radishes, lime, dried chiles, extra oregano, and optional toppings.
