## Ingredients

- Eggs
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/2 cup water
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- Honey
- Jalapeño, sliced
- Green onions, sliced
- Sesame seeds
- Cooked rice
- Kimchi

## Instructions

1. Boil the eggs for 6 minutes, then move to cold water and peel.
2. Mix the soy sauce, water, garlic, honey, jalapeño, green onions, and sesame seeds.
3. Put the eggs in a container with the marinade and refrigerate overnight.
4. Serve over leftover rice with a drizzle of the marinade and kimchi.

## Notes

- Skip drizzling the marinade if you're watching sodium.
