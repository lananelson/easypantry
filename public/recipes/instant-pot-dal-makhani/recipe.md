## Ingredients

- 1 cup black gram beans (or adzuki beans or whole brown lentils)
- 1/3 cup red kidney beans
- 1 tbsp oil, ghee, or butter
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
- 1 tbsp ginger paste
- 1 tbsp garlic paste
- 1/4 tsp ground turmeric
- 2 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
- 3 tsp kosher salt (2 tsp for the dal, 1 for the rice)
- 2 cups diced tomatoes
- 4 cups water (2 cups for the dal, 2 for the rice)
- 1/2 cup light cream
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/4 cup cilantro, chopped
- 1 tbsp kasoori methi (dried fenugreek leaves), optional
- 1 cup brown rice, rinsed and drained (optional)

## Instructions

1. Rinse and soak the black gram and kidney beans in 3 cups water overnight or 6–8 hours. Drain.
2. Set the Instant Pot to sauté and heat the oil. Add the cumin seeds and let them sizzle, then add the onion.
3. Add the ginger, garlic, turmeric, chili powder, 2 tsp salt, tomatoes, beans, and 2 cups water. Mix well.
4. For pot-in-pot rice (optional), put the rice, 2 cups water, and 1 tsp salt in a stainless steel container on a tall trivet over the beans.
5. Seal and pressure cook on high for 30 minutes, then let the pressure release naturally.
6. Lift out the rice and trivet. On sauté mode, stir in the cream and garam masala and garnish with cilantro and kasoori methi.

## Notes

- No time to soak: add 1 extra cup of water and pressure cook 60 minutes.
- Vegan: use oil instead of ghee, and coconut or cashew cream.
- Stovetop pressure cooker: 45 minutes on medium heat with natural release. Without a pressure cooker, simmer covered 2–3 hours, adding 1–2 cups water as needed.
