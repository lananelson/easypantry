## Ingredients

- 2 cups (approx. 500 ml) almond milk (or milk of your choice)
- 1 avocado (banana works too)
- 2 tbsp raw cacao
- 1 tsp vanilla extract (or seeds from 1/2 vanilla pod)
- 1-2 tbsp agave syrup
- pinch of sea salt
- 250 g raspberries, thawed if using frozen (raspberries)
- 2 tbsp agave syrup (raspberries)

## Instructions

1. Bring raspberries and agave syrup to a boil and cook for 4-5 minutes until soft and runny. Set aside. This can be made in larger batches and kept in the fridge for up to five days.
2. Mix all the ingredients for the smoothie to a creamy consistency in a blender.
3. Divide a couple spoonfuls of the raspberries between two glasses and pour the smoothie over. Have a spoon ready to scrape all the goodness out!

## Notes

- Serves 1 as a meal replacement during a fast/detox, or 2 as a snack between meals.
- For a more filling breakfast, add a couple spoonfuls of almond flour or soaked cashews.
