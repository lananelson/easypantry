## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp white wine vinegar (2 for the pickled jalapeños, 1 for the marinade)
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- Kosher salt
- 2 jalapeños (1 thinly sliced for pickling, 1 roughly chopped)
- 2/3 cup + 2 tbsp fresh cilantro
- 1/2 small red onion, thinly sliced
- Zest and juice of 2 limes
- 1 English cucumber
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp agave syrup
- 1 tsp yellow miso
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 can (15 oz) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 2 avocados
- 6 tostadas

## Instructions

1. Quick-pickle the jalapeños: mix 2 tbsp vinegar, the sugar, 1/2 tsp salt, the sliced jalapeño, and 2 tbsp minced cilantro.
2. In a big bowl, mellow the red onion with the lime juice and a pinch of salt. Chop the cucumber.
3. Cook the garlic in the olive oil until golden. Blend it with the lime zest, 1 tbsp vinegar, chopped jalapeño, agave, miso, and 2/3 cup cilantro.
4. Toss the marinade with the beans, cucumber, and onion. It gets better as it sits.
5. Serve on tostadas with avocado and the pickled jalapeños.
