## Ingredients

- 1 small spaghetti squash (about 600 g / 1 1/3 lb)
- 1 small head of garlic
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1 tsp chile flakes
- A handful of fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 1 1/2–2 cups mixed grated cheese, such as mozzarella or fontina with Parmesan, Gruyère, or sharp cheddar
- 300–400 g pasta, such as spaghetti or fettuccine
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- Kosher salt and pepper
- Parmesan, to serve

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 425°F (215°C) and line a medium baking sheet with parchment.
2. Cut the head of garlic in half crosswise, set the halves on the baking sheet, and drizzle about 2 tbsp olive oil over each. Halve the spaghetti squash through the middle (crosswise) and set each half cut side down on top of a garlic half, so the garlic steams and loses most of its pungency. Roast for 35–45 minutes.
3. Melt the butter in a large pan over medium heat. Squeeze the roasted garlic cloves into the butter and turn the heat to low. Cook gently for 3–5 minutes, until golden; add a drizzle of olive oil if the butter browns too fast. Mash the garlic with the back of a wooden spoon or a potato masher.
4. Add the chile flakes and sizzle for 1 minute. Scoop the squash straight into the pan with a fork, add a splash of water, and cook for 5 minutes.
5. Meanwhile, boil the pasta in generously salted water for 4 minutes less than the package says.
6. Add the pasta to the garlic butter squash with a splash of pasta water. Add the cheese and parsley and toss, adding more water as needed to loosen the sauce. Cook for 3 more minutes.
7. Serve right away with a drizzle of olive oil and freshly grated Parmesan.
