## Ingredients

- Salmon fillets
- Gochujang
- Miso
- Garlic, minced
- Soy sauce
- Mirin
- Sugar
- Sesame seeds
- Rice and lettuce leaves, to serve

## Instructions

1. Whisk together the gochujang, miso, garlic, soy sauce, mirin, and sugar.
2. Brush the glaze over the top and sides of the salmon.
3. Broil 5–10 minutes, depending on thickness.
4. Brush on more glaze and top with sesame seeds. Serve with rice in lettuce wraps.
