## Ingredients

- 2 Tbsp. olive oil
- 1 cup sliced onion
- 1 Tbsp. minced ginger
- 1 Tbsp. minced garlic
- 1 plum tomato, chopped
- 1 15-oz. can pumpkin puree
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1 cup unsweetened coconut milk
- 1 1/2 tsp. curry powder
- 1/8 tsp. cayenne pepper
- 1 cup butternut squash, roasted and diced
- 1 lb. shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 1/2 tsp. fresh lime juice
- Cilantro
- Lime zest

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add onion and ginger; sauté until soft, about 8 minutes. Add garlic; cook for 1 minute.
2. Stir in plum tomato and pumpkin puree; cook, stirring frequently, until pumpkin is golden brown, about 10 minutes.
3. Add vegetable broth, coconut milk, curry powder, and cayenne pepper; simmer for 20 minutes.
4. Add butternut squash, shrimp, and lime juice. Simmer until shrimp are cooked and squash is warm. Top with cilantro and lime zest.

## Notes

- Originally part of a meal plan: served with brown rice and carrot pilaf and broccoli rabe with chili-garlic black bean oil. Roast extra butternut squash for leftovers.
