## Ingredients

- 6 cups reduced-sodium chicken broth
- 2 cups water
- 1 1/3 cups small whole-wheat pasta
- 1 (15-ounce) can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 bunch scallions, sliced, whites and greens divided
- Dash of nutmeg
- 3 cups chopped spinach, Swiss chard, or arugula, stems removed
- 4 large eggs, lightly beaten
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice
- 6 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese

## Instructions

1. In a large pot or Dutch oven, combine broth, water, pasta, chickpeas, scallion whites, and nutmeg over high heat. Cover.
2. Once mixture comes to a boil, uncover and cook 3 to 4 minutes. Add leafy greens. Stir. Cook 1 minute.
3. Drop heat to a simmer. Slowly pour eggs into the soup, stirring constantly. Cook 2 minutes. Season with pepper.
4. Add scallion greens and lemon juice. Serve with Parmesan sprinkled on top.

## Notes

- Regular pasta works too; whole-wheat adds fiber. Just about any leafy green can be substituted.
