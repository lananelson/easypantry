## Ingredients

- 1 lb spicy Italian sausage
- 1 onion, diced (half for the meatballs, half for the sauce)
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 1/2 tbsp each ground ginger, cardamom, and white pepper
- 1/4 tsp allspice
- Fresh nutmeg
- 6 tbsp butter
- Egg noodles or pasta
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 cups beef broth
- 1 tbsp stone-ground mustard
- 1/2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- Sour cream or Greek yogurt, and parsley, to serve

## Instructions

1. Mix the sausage, half the onion, the garlic, breadcrumbs, egg, ginger, cardamom, white pepper, allspice, and nutmeg with your hands. Form into 1–2 inch meatballs.
2. Cook the meatballs in a large pan over medium heat with 3 tbsp butter. Keep warm.
3. In the same pan, add 3 more tbsp butter and the remaining onion. Meanwhile, cook the noodles or pasta.
4. Stir in the flour, then the broth, mustard, and Worcestershire.
5. Add the pasta and meatballs. Serve with sour cream or Greek yogurt and parsley.
