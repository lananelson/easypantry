## Ingredients

- 2 1/4 cups extra-virgin olive oil
- 8 ounces sweet sausage links, casings removed
- 8 ounces hot sausage links, casings removed
- 5 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 teaspoon to 1 tablespoon chile flakes, to taste
- 2 anchovies
- 2 bunches broccoli rabe, stems smashed, then roughly chopped
- 1 pound cavatelli (store-bought or homemade)
- 2 cups grated Parmesan
- 4 tablespoons (1/2 stick) salted butter

## Instructions

1. In a large sauté pan, add 2 cups olive oil. Add the sweet and hot sausage and cook, breaking up the meat so it browns all over, about 4 minutes.
2. Add the garlic, chile flakes, and anchovies and cook until just toasted.
3. Add the broccoli rabe and cook, stirring, for a few minutes.
4. Bring a pot of heavily salted water to a boil and add the cavatelli.
5. Add about 1 cup pasta water to the sausage mixture and use a wooden spoon to scrape up the fond from the bottom of the pan.
6. Pull the cavatelli just shy of al dente and finish cooking it in the sauce, tossing and adding more pasta water if needed if the mixture becomes too dry.
7. Stir in the grated Parmesan, butter, and remaining 1/4 cup olive oil.

## Notes

- Christian Petroni notes that this dish is traditionally associated with orecchiette, but here he uses cavatelli.
- The sliced garlic is intentionally toasted in the olive oil to give the dish its characteristic flavor.
