## Ingredients

- 1–1 1/2 lb (450–675 g) green beans, trimmed
- 2 lb small onions (9–10 onions, about 100 g each)
- 7 tbsp (98 g) unsalted butter, melted
- 7 tbsp (105 g) white or yellow miso
- 3 cups warm water
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- Salt, for blanching
- A little oil

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 500°F (260°C).
2. In a 9 × 13 inch baking dish, whisk the miso and melted butter until smooth, then whisk in the warm water.
3. Halve the onions lengthwise, trimming the tops and bottoms but leaving enough of the root to hold them together. Remove any papery layers.
4. Arrange the onions cut-side down in a single layer. Cover tightly with foil and bake 20 minutes.
5. Remove the foil, turn the onions cut-side up, and baste. Bake uncovered another 30–40 minutes, basting every 10 minutes or so, until tender and browned at the edges.
6. About 10 minutes before the onions are done, blanch the green beans in well-salted boiling water for 2–4 minutes, until crisp-tender and bright green. Shock in an ice bath, drain well, and toss in a shallow ovenproof dish with a little oil.
7. When the onions are ready, lower the oven to 350°F (175°C) and warm the green beans through.
8. Lift the onions out with a slotted spoon. Simmer the braising liquid in a small pot and whisk in the cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp cold water a little at a time, until it thickens to a thin gravy.
9. Arrange the onions over the green beans and pour the sauce over everything.

## Notes

- Use white or yellow miso; red miso is too strong here.
- The miso butter onions come from Ottolenghi's *Flavor* (Yotam Ottolenghi and Ixta Belfrage).
