## Ingredients

- One 13.5-ounce can full-fat coconut milk
- 2 ounces (approximately ¼ cup) Thai red curry paste (or Massaman, milder)
- ¾ cup unsweetened natural creamy peanut butter (no added sugar or emulsifiers)
- ½ tablespoon salt
- ¾ cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar or white vinegar (not wine, balsamic, or rice vinegar)
- ½ cup water

## Instructions

1. Put everything into a medium heavy-bottomed pot and bring to a very gentle boil over medium heat, whisking constantly.
2. Let the mixture simmer for 3-5 minutes over low heat; be careful not to let it scorch on the bottom.
3. Take the pot off the heat, let the sauce cool to room temperature (or slightly warmer), and serve with satay or fried tofu.

## Notes

- This is a Thai satay sauce — sweet, with a curry flavor but not hot. It's not a Chinese-style noodle sauce or a Vietnamese spring-roll sauce.
- Keeps in a glass container in the fridge for weeks; it thickens, so thin with a little water and reheat. Freezes beautifully.
- The author prefers Maesri red curry paste; Mae Ploy is hotter.
- No natural peanut butter available: grind 12 ounces roasted peanuts into a paste and use the same way.
