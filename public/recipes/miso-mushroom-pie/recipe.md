## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 leek
- 300g shiitake mushrooms
- 500g chestnut mushrooms
- 500g oyster mushrooms
- 6 cloves of garlic
- 8 sprigs of thyme
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 150g cashew nuts
- 200ml plant-based milk
- 2 tbsp miso paste
- 750g puff pastry
- 15ml plant-based milk

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Prepare the filling ingredients: wash and thinly slice the leek, finely slice the shiitake and chestnut mushrooms, tear the oyster mushrooms, peel and crush the garlic, and remove the thyme leaves from the stalks.
2. Make the filling: in a cast iron frying pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Sauté the leek with a pinch of salt for 4–5 minutes until starting to soften. Increase the heat to medium-high and add the shiitake, chestnut, and oyster mushrooms. Sauté, stirring occasionally, until the mushrooms are soft and there is no moisture left in the pan, about 10 minutes. Stir in the garlic, thyme, and soy sauce and cook for 1 minute.
3. Meanwhile, make the cashew béchamel: bring a saucepan of water to a boil, add the cashews, and reduce the heat to medium. Simmer for 10 minutes to soften the cashews, then drain and rinse under cold running water. Add them to a high-speed blender with the plant-based milk and miso paste and blend until smooth, adding more milk as needed to reach a pourable consistency.
4. Finish the pie and bake: take the mushroom filling off the heat, pour the cashew béchamel over, and stir to combine. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Transfer to a pie dish if needed, lay the puff pastry over the filling, fork around the edges for decoration, and tuck the pastry down the sides to create a tight seal. Pierce two small air holes in the top to stop the filling from bursting. Brush the pastry with the plant-based milk. Bake for 20–25 minutes until the pastry is golden brown. Serve with your favourite vegetables.

## Notes

- Cook the mushrooms until there is no moisture left in the pan.
- Pierce two small air holes in the pastry so the filling doesn't burst while baking.
