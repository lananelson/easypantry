## Ingredients

- Heads of spring garlic
- Olive oil
- Bread, toast or crackers, to serve

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F and have a baking dish ready.
2. Peel the first layer around the heads of garlic and slice the top of each head to reveal the cloves.
3. Place the garlic heads in the dish and drizzle each with olive oil.
4. Cover with foil and bake for 50 minutes, or until the cloves are soft when you press with your finger. Remove from the oven and allow to cool.
5. To eat, spread on bread, toast or crackers.
