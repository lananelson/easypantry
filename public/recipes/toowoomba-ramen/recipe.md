## Ingredients

- Instant ramen, with its seasoning packet
- Bacon
- Shrimp or another meat (optional)
- 3–4 cloves garlic
- Onion, sliced
- Oil
- Whole milk
- Cheddar cheese (or mozzarella)
- Green onion, to garnish

## Instructions

1. Cook the bacon first, then fry the shrimp or other meat, garlic, and onion in the bacon fat until cooked.
2. Add whole milk and as much of the ramen seasoning packet as you like, and bring to a boil.
3. Meanwhile, cook the noodles in a separate pot.
4. Add the noodles to the sauce and mix. Add the cheese and stir until melted; the sauce should look a little orange, not red.
5. Mix once more, plate, and sprinkle with green onion. Eat right away, before the noodles soften.
