## Ingredients

- 6 chicken thighs
- 2 tbsp chipotle pepper sauce
- 2 tsp salt, plus more to taste
- 2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp dried parsley flakes
- 1 1/2 tsp cumin
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp chili powder
- Juice of 1 lime, plus lime wedges to serve
- Juice of 1/2 orange
- 1 1/2 onions (1/2 sliced for the marinade, 1 piece for the salsa, the rest for toppings)
- Cilantro (a handful each for marinade and salsa, plus more chopped to serve)
- 10 garlic cloves (5 crushed for the marinade, 5 for the salsa)
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 2 jalapeños
- 1 avocado
- 1/2 cup water
- Corn tortillas

## Instructions

1. Marinate the chicken with the chipotle sauce, salt, pepper, parsley, cumin, paprika, chili powder, lime and orange juice, sliced onion, a handful of cilantro, crushed garlic, and 1 tbsp olive oil. Refrigerate at least 1 hour or overnight.
2. For the salsa, fry the jalapeños, a piece of onion, and the garlic in 1 tbsp oil until toasted. Blend with a handful of cilantro, the avocado, water, and salt until creamy.
3. Pan-fry the chicken in 2 tbsp oil about 7 minutes per side, until cooked through with grill marks. Rest 10 minutes, then chop small.
4. Fry the tortillas in the same pan. Caramelize some sliced onion and finely chop the rest with cilantro.
5. Top the tortillas with chicken, onion and cilantro, salsa, and a squeeze of lime.
