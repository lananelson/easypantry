## Ingredients

- Artichokes
- Lemon
- Olive oil
- Salt
- Butter
- Garlic

## Instructions

1. Snip the prickly tips off the leaves and peel off the tough bottom leaves. Slice off the top and trim the end of the stem.
2. Cut in half, cut along the edges of the fuzzy choke with a small knife, and scoop it out. Rinse and dry.
3. Squeeze lemon juice over the cut sides, drizzle with olive oil, and sprinkle with salt.
4. Bake at 375°F for 18–20 minutes.
5. Melt butter with garlic and salt for dipping. Pull off the leaves and scrape the tender ends with your teeth.
