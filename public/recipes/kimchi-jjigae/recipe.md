## Ingredients

- 3/4 lb pork belly, sliced
- Salt
- 1/2 onion, sliced
- 3 scallions (whites for cooking, greens to garnish)
- 1 lb well-fermented kimchi
- 1/4 cup kimchi juice
- 4 1/2 cups dashi stock
- 1/2 container soft tofu
- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp gochugaru flakes
- 1 tbsp gochugaru powder
- 2 tsp sugar
- 3 cloves garlic, grated
- 1/2-inch knob ginger, grated
- Rice, to serve

## Instructions

1. Slice the onion, scallions, kimchi, and pork belly.
2. Mix the gochujang, soy sauce, sesame oil, both gochugaru, sugar, garlic, and ginger.
3. In a large stone pot over medium heat, cook the pork belly until it starts to release its fat.
4. Stir-fry the kimchi in the fat for a couple of minutes, then add the onion and scallion whites and cook until the onion is slightly translucent.
5. Add the sauce, kimchi juice, and dashi and simmer 10–15 minutes.
6. Add the tofu, cover, and simmer a few minutes more.
7. Garnish with scallion greens and serve with rice.

## Notes

- Use well-fermented kimchi; fresh kimchi won't taste as good here.
