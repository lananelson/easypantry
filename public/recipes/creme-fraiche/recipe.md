## Ingredients

- 1 pint heavy cream
- 2 tablespoons cultured buttermilk

## Instructions

1. Combine buttermilk and heavy cream in a non-reactive container. Cover and allow to rest at room temperature until thickened to desired texture, about 12 hours.
2. Store in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
