## Ingredients

- Ciabatta or a crusty roll
- Pistachio pesto
- Mortadella
- Stracciatella
- Hard cheese, grated

## Instructions

1. Split the bread and toast the cut sides in a pan.
2. Spread with pistachio pesto and add grated cheese.
3. Layer on mortadella and top with stracciatella.
4. Close the sandwich and cut in half.
