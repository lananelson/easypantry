## Ingredients

- 1 shallot
- 6 garlic cloves
- 4 Roma tomatoes
- 4 quarts water
- 1/3 cup dried shrimp
- 1 yellow onion
- 2-inch nub ginger
- 2 lemongrass stalks
- 15 oz pineapple chunks
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- 1-inch nub rock sugar
- 1/2 tbsp MSG
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 tbsp oil
- 30 oz canned sardines in tomato sauce
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tbsp minced shallots
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 lb rice vermicelli
- 3 quarts water
- 1/2 tbsp salt
- 1 tbsp oil
- 16 oz fish balls
- Cabbage
- Lettuce
- Green onions
- Cilantro
- Limes
- Fried shallots
- Black pepper

## Instructions

1. Soak dried shrimp in warm water for 5–10 minutes and drain. Halve the onion and ginger; trim and bruise lemongrass; wedge tomatoes. Prepare cabbage, lettuce, scallions, cilantro and lime garnishes.
2. Mince shallot and garlic in a food processor, reserving 2 tablespoons for sardines. Blend half the tomatoes with remaining aromatics.
3. Cook the tomato mixture in oil for 2–3 minutes. Add 4 quarts water, dried shrimp, onion, ginger, lemongrass and pineapple. Bring to a boil, skim, then simmer partly covered for 30–45 minutes. Remove onion, ginger and lemongrass.
4. Sauté reserved aromatics in oil for 1–2 minutes. Add sardines and their tomato sauce, warming gently for 3–4 minutes. Set sardines aside and transfer sauce to broth.
5. Cook fish balls in broth according to package directions, about 10 minutes. Add fish sauce, rock sugar and MSG. Simmer remaining tomato wedges for 2 minutes and adjust seasoning.
6. Boil rice vermicelli in 3 quarts salted water according to package instructions. Drain, rinse with cold water and toss with oil.
7. Serve noodles with cabbage, lettuce, broth, fish balls and sardines. Garnish with green onions, cilantro, fried shallots, pepper and lime.
