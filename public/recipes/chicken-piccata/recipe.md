## Ingredients

- 3 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- All-purpose flour, for lightly coating
- 2 tbsp olive oil, for frying
- 2 tbsp capers
- 1/2 cup chicken stock
- 1/2 cup white wine
- Salt and black pepper
- 250 g unsalted butter
- 4–6 lemons, juiced
- 3 large garlic cloves, peeled and crushed
- 100 g spaghetti
- Fresh parsley, chopped

## Instructions

1. Make the lemon butter: melt the butter with the crushed garlic, only as long as it takes the butter to melt if you like a strong garlic taste. Combine with the lemon juice and blend until smooth.
2. Put the chicken between layers of cling film or in a large zip-lock bag and flatten it with a meat mallet or the bottom of a heavy saucepan. Season both sides with salt and pepper and leave uncovered at room temperature for about 30 minutes (a short dry brine).
3. Lightly coat the chicken in flour. Heat a generous amount of olive oil in a pan and cook the chicken until browned on both sides, 3–4 minutes a side. Set aside.
4. Drain the oil from the pan and put it back over high heat. Deglaze with the chicken stock and white wine and reduce by half.
5. Stir in 2 tbsp of the lemon butter and the capers. Return the chicken to the pan and coat it in the sauce. Check the seasoning only now, since the capers add a lot of salt, then finish with plenty of black pepper.
6. Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti. Toss it with 2–3 tbsp lemon butter, 1–2 tbsp of the pasta cooking water, and chopped parsley. Serve with the chicken.

## Notes

- The recipe is per serving; scale the chicken, sauce, and spaghetti per person.
- The lemon butter makes a big batch and keeps for about a week in the fridge. It's equal parts unsalted butter and lemon juice by volume, so it's easy to make a smaller batch.
- Piccata is usually made with chicken breast; he uses thighs.
