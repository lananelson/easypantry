## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup white sugar (for dusting pans)
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon (for dusting pans)
- 3/4 cup butter
- 3 cups white sugar
- 3 eggs
- 6 very ripe bananas, mashed
- 1 (16 ounce) container sour cream
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3 teaspoons baking soda
- 4 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup chopped walnuts (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 300 degrees F (150 degrees C). Grease four 7x3 inch loaf pans. In a small bowl, stir together 1/4 cup white sugar and 1 teaspoon cinnamon. Dust pans lightly with cinnamon and sugar mixture.
2. In a large bowl, cream butter and 3 cups sugar. Mix in eggs, mashed bananas, sour cream, vanilla and cinnamon. Mix in salt, baking soda and flour. Stir in nuts. Divide into prepared pans.
3. Bake for 1 hour, until a toothpick inserted in center comes out clean.

## Notes

- Reader tip: A dash of nutmeg and ground cloves is a nice addition.
- Reader tip: Halved recipe in two 7 1/2" x 9" loaves: bake at 325°F for 1 hr 10 min (at 300°F they came out underdone).
- Reader tip: The butter and sugar won't really "cream" — too much sugar — but don't reduce it.
- Reader tip: Hand-mix the flour in for a denser, more bread-like loaf; an electric mixer makes it cakier.
- Reader tip: Crumb topping for two loaves: ~1/2 cup packed brown sugar, 1/2 cup flour, 2-3 tbsp softened butter, 1 tsp cinnamon, 1/3 cup chopped walnuts or pecans, mixed with a fork until crumbly and spooned on before baking.
