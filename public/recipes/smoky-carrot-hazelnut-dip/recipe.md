## Ingredients

- 6–7 large carrots
- 7 tbsp olive oil, plus good extra-virgin olive oil to finish
- 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 3/4 tsp nutmeg
- Sea salt
- 2/3 cup hazelnuts, plus more chopped to garnish
- 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, with their liquid (aquafaba)
- 6 tbsp orange juice (about 1 large orange)
- 8–10 sage leaves
- Chili flakes or cayenne, to garnish

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Rinse the carrots, trim the stems, and chop them into 2–3 inch pieces.
2. On a sheet pan, toss the carrots with 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp cinnamon, 1/2 tsp nutmeg, and a generous pinch of sea salt. Roast for 25–30 minutes, until fork-tender.
3. For the last 5 minutes of the carrots' roast, roast the hazelnuts on another tray. Let them cool, then rub off the skins: shake them in a jar or container until the nuts are bare.
4. Drain the chickpeas, saving the aquafaba.
5. In a food processor or high-speed blender, combine the roasted carrots, chickpeas, hazelnuts, orange juice, 2 tbsp olive oil, and 2 tbsp aquafaba. Season with 1/2 tsp sea salt, 1/2 tsp cinnamon, and 1/4 tsp nutmeg.
6. Blend until smooth, 3–4 minutes. Add 1–2 more tbsp of chilled aquafaba or water if it needs loosening. Taste and adjust the salt, cinnamon, and nutmeg.
7. For the crispy sage, heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium-low heat and toast the sage leaves until slightly crispy. Drain on a paper towel and sprinkle with salt.
8. Spread the dip in a dish and drizzle with extra-virgin olive oil. Top with the crispy sage, chopped hazelnuts, and a sprinkle of chili or cayenne. Serve with crusty bread.

## Notes

- Skinning the hazelnuts is an extra step, but it gives a smoother, restaurant-style dip.
