## Ingredients

- Fresh green beans
- Oil, for deep-frying
- Sesame oil
- Hoisin sauce
- Soy sauce
- Chili crisp

## Instructions

1. Deep-fry the green beans for 2–3 minutes, until blistered and wrinkled. Drain on paper towels.
2. In a pan, warm the sesame oil with the hoisin, soy sauce, and chili crisp.
3. Add the green beans and toss lightly to coat.
