## Ingredients

- Yellow onions, thinly sliced
- Butter or oil
- Broth
- Rigatoni
- Hard cheese, grated
- Fennel and microgreens, to serve (optional)

## Instructions

1. Slowly caramelize the onions in a pot until deep golden brown.
2. Pour in broth and scrape up the browned bits.
3. Add the rigatoni and cook it in the broth until tender and saucy.
4. Serve topped with grated cheese, with a shaved fennel salad on the side if you like.
