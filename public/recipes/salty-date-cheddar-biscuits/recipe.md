## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup (1 stick / 113 g) cold unsalted butter, plus more for serving
- 6 ounces extra-sharp cheddar cheese
- 1 cup plus 3 tablespoons sour cream
- 1 egg
- 6 ounces Medjool dates (about 1 cup)
- 2 cups (240 g) all-purpose flour, plus more as needed
- 2 tablespoons (24 g) sugar
- 2 teaspoons (6 g) kosher salt
- 1 1/2 teaspoons (6 g) baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon (3 g) baking soda
- Freshly ground black pepper
- Flaky sea salt

## Instructions

1. Position a rack in the center of the oven. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Cut the cold butter into 1/2-inch cubes. Transfer to a small bowl and chill in the fridge until ready to use.
3. Remove and discard the pits from the Medjool dates. Coarsely chop the dates into 1/2-inch pieces, flouring your knife if they are very sticky.
4. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, kosher salt, baking powder, baking soda, and a few generous cranks of black pepper.
5. Set a box grater over the bowl and grate the cheddar on the large holes. Toss with your hands to combine.
6. Add the chilled butter pieces and, using your fingers, pinch and squeeze the butter into the flour until there are no pieces larger than a pea.
7. Add the chopped dates and toss to combine, using your fingers to separate any pieces that have clumped together.
8. Make a well in the center of the flour. Scrape in the sour cream and, using a fork, stir in circular motions until the dough comes together in large clumps.
9. Using lightly floured hands, knead the dough a few times in the bowl to bring it together into a shaggy mass and incorporate any flour at the bottom.
10. Transfer the dough to a lightly floured surface. Knead once or twice more with the palms of your hands to form a more uniform mass while keeping some shagginess.
11. Pat the dough into an 8 x 4-inch rectangle. Fold the short edges toward the center and let them overlap, like a tri-fold business letter. Pat the dough back down into another 8 x 4-inch rectangle.
12. Cut the rectangle in half lengthwise, then slice it crosswise into 4 equal pieces to create 8 squares. Transfer to the parchment-lined baking sheet, spacing them evenly.
13. Lightly beat the egg in a small bowl. Brush the tops of the biscuits with the egg wash and sprinkle with flaky sea salt.
14. Bake, rotating the baking sheet front to back halfway through, until lightly golden brown on top, 18 to 22 minutes.
15. Serve warm with more butter and flaky sea salt.

## Notes

- Molly emphasizes keeping the ingredients as cold as possible so shards of butter remain intact in the dough and turn to steam in the oven, helping create flaky layers.
- Avoid overworking the dough; it should remain somewhat shaggy rather than becoming completely smooth.
- Rotating the baking sheet helps compensate for uneven oven heat and promotes even browning.
