## Ingredients

- 1/2–1 lb flank steak (or other beef), thinly sliced
- 3 tbsp oyster sauce (1 for the marinade, 2 for the sauce)
- 1 tbsp soy sauce (1/2 for the marinade, 1/2 for the sauce)
- 1 tsp + 1 1/2 tbsp dark soy sauce (1 tsp for the marinade, 1 tbsp for the sauce, 1/2 tbsp for the noodles)
- 1 tsp + 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 2 lb fresh wide rice noodles (1 pack)
- 1/2 lb Chinese broccoli
- 3 eggs
- 1 tbsp sweet soy sauce
- 1/2 tbsp seasoning sauce (or soy sauce)
- 2–3 tbsp canola oil
- Garlic, thinly sliced

## Instructions

1. Marinate the beef with 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 1/2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp dark soy, 1 tsp sugar, 1/2 tsp pepper, and the baking soda while you prep.
2. For the sauce, mix 2 tbsp oyster sauce, the sweet soy, 1 tbsp dark soy, 1 tbsp sugar, 1/2 tbsp soy sauce, the seasoning sauce, and 1 tsp pepper.
3. Slice the Chinese broccoli on a bias about 1/4 inch thick. Optionally steam it with a splash of water in the microwave for 1 minute, uncovered.
4. Separate the rice noodles into long strands (rubbing them against a cutting board helps). Toss with 1/2 tbsp dark soy to coat.
5. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat. Sauté the garlic and beef until about 90% cooked.
6. Add the noodles and cook 2–3 minutes to char lightly, scooping from underneath so they don't break.
7. Push aside, scramble the eggs over medium-high until 80% done, then toss everything together.
8. Add the Chinese broccoli and sauce and toss about 2 minutes, until evenly coated.
