## Ingredients

- 5 tsp Sichuan peppercorns
- 1½ tsp whole white peppercorns (optional)
- 6 tbsp spicy chili crisp, preferably Lao Gan Ma
- ⅓ cup chili oil
- 3 tbsp light soy sauce
- 3 tbsp unrefined peanut oil
- 5 tsp Chinkiang black vinegar
- 12 oz glass noodles made from sweet potato starch or mung beans
- 2 celery stalks, sliced into thin 2-inch pieces
- 1 lb shredded cooked turkey (about 3 cups)
- Leaves from 1 small bunch basil, preferably Thai or Opal basil
- Leaves and tender stems from 1 small bunch cilantro
- ½ cup crushed roasted peanuts, salted or unsalted

## Instructions

1. Dry-toast both types of peppercorns over medium heat for about 2 minutes, until aromatic. Crush them coarsely in a mortar or spice grinder and whisk them with the chili crisp, chili oil, soy sauce, peanut oil, and vinegar.
2. Boil the noodles as directed on the package. Add the celery for the final 2 minutes. Drain, rinse with cold water to cool, and shake off excess water before portioning into bowls.
3. Put the shredded turkey over the noodles. Stir the dressing again and spoon it over each serving, then scatter the basil, cilantro, and peanuts on top.

## Notes

- Bon Appétit suggests starting with less chili oil if yours runs especially hot. A sesame-based chili oil is preferred if available.
