## Ingredients

- Shell-on shrimp
- Small potatoes
- Corn on the cob, cut into pieces
- Sausage
- Eggs
- Onion
- Lemons
- Salt
- Paprika
- Mustard powder
- Butter
- Tabasco

## Instructions

1. Mix the salt, paprika, and mustard powder for the seasoning.
2. Bring a large pot of water to a boil with some of the seasoning, squeezed lemons, and onion.
3. Boil the potatoes, corn, sausage, and eggs until the potatoes are tender, then add the shrimp and cook until pink.
4. Melt butter with the rest of the seasoning and Tabasco.
5. Drain the boil and toss everything with the spicy butter.
