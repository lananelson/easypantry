## Ingredients

- 3 small round shallots
- 150 g dried lentils
- 500 g heirloom tomatoes
- 1 peach
- Olive oil for charring (not extra virgin)
- 150 g sourdough bread (or ciabatta)
- 1 small garlic clove
- 6 tbsp extra virgin olive oil, plus more to finish
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 20 g basil
- Salt and black pepper

## Instructions

1. Peel the shallots, slice them thinly, and soak the slices in ice water. This takes away the harsh bite and makes them crisp.
2. Meanwhile, put the lentils in a pan with roughly double their volume of water and 3/4 tsp salt. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer for about 20 minutes until tender, adding more water if needed. Drain and set aside.
3. Cut the tomatoes into bite-sized pieces. Halve and pit the peach, then cut each half into quarters. Brush the quarters with a little olive oil and grill them in a griddle pan, turning once, until both sides are nicely charred. Set aside.
4. Tear the bread into small chunks, toss with a little olive oil, and grill in the same pan until lightly charred all over. Set aside.
5. Finely crush the garlic into a bowl large enough for the panzanella. Add the extra virgin olive oil, vinegar, and 1/3 tsp salt and stir.
6. Add the lentils, tomatoes, charred bread, peaches, and most of the shallots. Tear in most of the basil and add a generous grind of black pepper. Mix everything together.
7. Top with the remaining shallots and basil and a generous drizzle of extra virgin olive oil.

## Notes

- If you're serving it the next day, keep the bread separate and out of the fridge so it doesn't go soggy and lose its char.
