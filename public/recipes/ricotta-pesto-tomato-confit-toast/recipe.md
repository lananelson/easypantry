## Ingredients

- Cherry tomatoes
- Garlic cloves
- Chili flakes
- Olive oil
- Sourdough bread
- Salt
- Fresh basil
- Parmesan, grated
- Ricotta

## Instructions

1. Cover cherry tomatoes and garlic cloves with olive oil and a pinch of chili flakes, and roast slowly until the garlic is soft and the tomatoes burst.
2. Toast the sourdough in a pan with some of the confit oil.
3. Pound the confit garlic with salt in a mortar, then add the basil and Parmesan and pound into a pesto, loosening with confit oil.
4. Stir the pesto into ricotta.
5. Spread the garlic and the ricotta pesto on the toast and top with the confit tomatoes and a pinch of salt.
