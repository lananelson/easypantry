## Ingredients

- Potatoes
- 1 stick butter, plus a pat to serve
- 30 cloves garlic
- A little flour
- 1 cup milk, plus a little more
- Salt and white pepper
- A splash of cream
- Parsley, chopped

## Instructions

1. Cook the garlic in the butter on the lowest heat until soft, without letting the butter brown.
2. Start the potatoes in cold water and cook until soft. Drain and let them steam dry, then rice them.
3. Blend the garlic and butter and return it to the pot. Whisk in a little flour for a couple of minutes, then slowly add the milk with salt and white pepper and cook about 5 minutes.
4. Fold the garlic sauce into the potatoes with a splash of cream.
5. Top with a pat of butter and parsley.
