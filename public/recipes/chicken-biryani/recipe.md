## Ingredients

- Chicken, cut into small pieces
- 1 spoon ginger-garlic paste
- Turmeric
- Red chili powder
- Garam masala
- Salt
- 1/2 lemon, juiced, plus more lemon juice to finish
- A little yogurt
- 1 glass basmati rice
- Bay leaf
- Green cardamom
- Oil
- Cumin seeds
- Black cardamom
- Cinnamon
- 1 red onion, sliced
- Tomato
- Green chili
- Saffron, soaked in a little milk
- Mint leaves
- Cilantro
- Crispy fried onions
- Ghee

## Instructions

1. Mix the chicken with the ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, red chili powder, garam masala, salt, lemon juice, and yogurt. Marinate 30 minutes.
2. Boil the rice with bay leaf, green cardamom, and salt until cooked, and drain.
3. In a pot, heat oil with cumin seeds, black cardamom, and cinnamon. Add the onion, then the tomato and green chili.
4. Add the chicken and enough water to cover. Cook 10–15 minutes.
5. Soak the saffron in a little milk for a couple of minutes.
6. Layer the rice over the chicken. Top with mint, cilantro, crispy onions, ghee, the saffron milk, and a squeeze of lemon.
7. Cover and cook a few more minutes on low heat.
