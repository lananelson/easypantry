## Ingredients

- 1 bundle rice noodles
- 1 lb shrimp
- 1 tsp granulated garlic
- 1 tsp white pepper
- 1/2 pack firm tofu, cubed
- 3 green onions
- 1 shallot
- 4 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp palm sugar (or brown sugar)
- 1/4 cup tamarind paste (or 2 tbsp tomato paste + 1 tbsp vinegar)
- 1/4 cup fish sauce
- 1 tsp chicken bouillon powder
- 2 eggs
- Oil
- 1 cup bean sprouts
- 1–2 tsp chili flakes (optional)
- Roasted peanuts, crushed, and lime wedges, to serve

## Instructions

1. Soak the rice noodles in room-temperature water while you prep, so they cook evenly. Chop the tofu, aromatics, and palm sugar. Season the shrimp with the granulated garlic and white pepper.
2. For the sauce, cook the shallot and garlic in a little oil until golden. Add the palm sugar, tamarind, fish sauce, and bouillon and heat until combined. Set aside.
3. In a little more oil, lay the shrimp flat; once pink at the edges, flip and cook 30 seconds more. Remove.
4. Cook the tofu until golden. Meanwhile, drain the noodles and pour boiling water over them for about a minute.
5. Add the noodles to the tofu, pour in the sauce, and let the noodles soak it up.
6. Push the noodles aside, scramble the eggs in the empty space, then toss them through.
7. Return the shrimp with the green onions and bean sprouts and toss for a minute.
8. Serve with crushed peanuts, more green onion, chili flakes, and lime.

## Notes

- The sauce can be made ahead in a big batch and kept in the fridge.
