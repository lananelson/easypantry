## Ingredients

- 270 g sourdough discard
- 720 g flour (550 g + 170 g)
- 500 g milk
- 30 g sugar
- 10 g baking soda
- Cornmeal or semolina, for the trays

## Instructions

1. The night before, stir together the discard, 550 g flour, and milk in a stand mixer bowl just until mixed. Cover and let rest overnight, 8–18 hours.
2. The next day, add 170 g flour, the sugar, and the baking soda and mix into a dough.
3. Roll out about 1/4 to 1/2 inch thick. Cut out circles, pushing the cutter straight down without twisting.
4. Set them on trays dusted with cornmeal.
5. Cook in a skillet or on a griddle until browned on both sides and cooked through.
