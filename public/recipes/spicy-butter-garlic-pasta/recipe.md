## Ingredients

- Pasta
- Salt, for the pasta water
- Olive oil
- Garlic, sliced
- Butter
- Chili flakes
- Oyster sauce
- Soy sauce
- Chives
- Parmesan
- Sesame seeds, to serve
- Crispy chili oil, to serve (optional)

## Instructions

1. Salt a pot of boiling water and cook the pasta.
2. Meanwhile, in a separate pan over low heat, cook the olive oil and garlic slices until the garlic is crispy and golden. Lift out the garlic chips and set them aside.
3. Add butter to the garlic oil, then the chili flakes, oyster sauce, and soy sauce, and mix it all together.
4. About 3 minutes before the pasta is al dente, transfer it to the pan to finish cooking there. Add some pasta water and toss.
5. Add the chives and parmesan and toss again until evenly coated.
6. Serve topped with sesame seeds, crispy chili oil if you like, and the crispy golden garlic chips.
