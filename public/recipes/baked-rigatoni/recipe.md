## Ingredients

- Canned whole tomatoes
- Olive oil
- Onion
- Rigatoni
- Ricotta
- Fresh mozzarella
- Parmesan
- Fresh basil

## Instructions

1. Make a tomato sauce: cook onion in olive oil and simmer with the canned tomatoes.
2. Boil the rigatoni until just short of done, drain, and toss with sauce.
3. Spread in a baking dish, spoon more sauce on top, and dot with ricotta, torn mozzarella, grated Parmesan, and basil.
4. Bake until bubbling and browned on top.
