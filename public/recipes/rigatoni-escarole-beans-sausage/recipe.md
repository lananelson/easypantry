## Ingredients

- Rigatoni
- Hot Italian sausage, casings removed
- Escarole
- Cannellini beans
- Calabrian chili
- Pecorino Romano, grated
- Parsley, to garnish

## Instructions

1. Brown the crumbled sausage in a pan.
2. Add the escarole and beans with a little Calabrian chili and cook until the escarole wilts.
3. Toss in the cooked rigatoni with a splash of pasta water.
4. Finish with plenty of grated pecorino and a sprig of parsley.
