## Ingredients

- 1 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 cup garlic cloves, peeled and roots removed
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter, room temperature
- 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan
- 2 tsp flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt (Diamond Crystal), or to taste
- 1 loaf French bread, cut into 1-inch diagonal slices

## Instructions

1. Put the olive oil and garlic in a small saucepan, adding more oil if needed to submerge the cloves. Bring to a simmer, then lower to medium-low so the cloves are surrounded by tiny bubbles rather than frying.
2. Cook about 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the cloves are light golden. Let cool to room temperature.
3. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Lift out the garlic with a slotted spoon, letting the oil drain back into the pan.
4. Mash the garlic with the butter, Parmesan, parsley, and salt until well combined.
5. Spread on the bread slices, set on a baking sheet, and bake about 7 minutes, until golden.

## Notes

- Keep the leftover garlic oil in a sterile jar in the fridge for up to 5 days.
