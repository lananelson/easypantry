## Ingredients

- 1 large white or yellow onion
- 7 garlic cloves
- 2 lemons
- 4 ounces sugar snap peas
- 1 large bunch dill or cilantro
- 2 pounds bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 6 small)
- 1 1/2 cups basmati rice
- Kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 3/4 cup roasted, salted pistachios
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 cup plain whole-milk yogurt

## Instructions

1. Place the basmati rice in a medium bowl and cover with room-temperature water. Let soak for 30 minutes.
2. Pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels. Season all over with 2 teaspoons salt and a few good cranks of black pepper.
3. Arrange the thighs skin-side down in a large, dry Dutch oven. Set over medium heat and cook, undisturbed, until the skin releases easily from the pot and is light golden brown, 8 to 10 minutes.
4. Once the skin no longer sticks, move the chicken around in the pot to encourage even browning and continue to cook until the skin is very crisp and deeply golden brown, 6 to 8 minutes longer. Transfer the thighs to a plate, skin-side up.
5. While the chicken cooks, finely chop the onion and 6 garlic cloves.
6. Drain the soaked rice in a fine-mesh strainer, rinsing with cold water and swirling the rice until the water runs completely clear, 1 to 2 minutes. Set the strainer on a kitchen towel to drain any additional water.
7. Reduce the heat under the Dutch oven, which should now contain only the rendered chicken fat, to medium-low. Stir in the onion and cook, stirring often, until translucent and soft, about 6 minutes.
8. Add the chopped garlic and cook, stirring, until fragrant, about 1 minute. Season with salt and lots of black pepper.
9. Stir in the rice and cook, stirring constantly, until the grains turn translucent around the edges, about 1 minute, toasting the rice in the chicken fat.
10. Add 2 cups water, the butter, and 1 teaspoon salt and stir to scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot. Bring the water to a boil over medium-high heat.
11. Once boiling, nestle the chicken thighs back into the pot skin-side up, along with any accumulated juices. Cover, reduce the heat to low, and cook without removing the lid for exactly 16 minutes. Immediately turn off the heat and let the rice finish steaming, covered, for 10 minutes.
12. Finely grate the zest of 1 lemon and the remaining garlic clove into a small bowl. Stir in the yogurt. Cut the lemon in half and squeeze in the juice of one half. Season the yogurt with salt.
13. Cut the snap peas crosswise into 1-inch pieces. Coarsely chop the pistachios. Finely chop the leaves and tender stems from the dill or cilantro, discarding the tough stems. Cut the remaining lemon in half.
14. Transfer the chicken to a plate. Fluff the rice with a fork.
15. Add the snap peas, chopped herbs, pistachios, and the juice of the halved lemon, fluffing with a fork as you incorporate them. Taste and adjust the seasoning as needed.
16. Divide the rice and chicken among shallow serving bowls or plates. Spoon some of the lemony yogurt over the top and finish with a few cranks of black pepper.

## Notes

- Molly describes this as one-pot comfort food: the chicken thighs steam over garlicky basmati rice, while their rendered fat seasons the rice as it cooks.
- Soaking and rinsing the basmati removes excess surface starch and helps the grains cook evenly and stay fluffy rather than gluey.
- The recipe specifically recommends good-quality basmati rice because the rice and chicken cook together in the same pot and need to finish at the same time.
