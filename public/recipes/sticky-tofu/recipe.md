## Ingredients

- Firm or extra-firm tofu
- Garlic, minced
- Chili, diced
- Ginger
- Soy sauce
- Mirin
- Sesame oil
- Gochujang
- Good-quality honey
- A splash of boiling water
- Potato starch
- Vegetable oil, for frying
- Rice, to serve
- Sesame seeds and green onions, to garnish

## Instructions

1. Drain as much water from the tofu as possible: press it between paper towels or a dish cloth (or on a drying rack) under something heavy for about 30 minutes, then cut it into cubes.
2. For the sauce, combine the garlic, chili, and ginger with the soy sauce, mirin, sesame oil, gochujang, and a little honey. Add a tiny bit of boiling water to bring it together.
3. While the rice cooks, coat the tofu in potato starch, which crisps better than flour.
4. Fry the tofu over high heat in vegetable oil until crispy, moving it around as little as possible.
5. Add the sauce and cook for 1 more minute, until sticky.
6. Serve over rice, garnished with sesame seeds and green onions.
