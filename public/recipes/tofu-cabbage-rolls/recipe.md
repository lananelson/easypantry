## Ingredients

- 1 head napa cabbage
- 1 tbsp oil, for sautéing
- 280 g extra-firm tofu
- 1/2 cup cooked rice
- 140 g mushrooms, chopped
- 140 g carrot, chopped
- 2 green onions, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, grated
- 1/2 tsp ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp + 1 tsp cornstarch
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 2 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1/4 tsp white pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat. Sauté the mushrooms for 5–7 minutes, until softened and most of their water has evaporated.
2. Add the carrots, green onions, garlic, and ginger and sauté about 5 minutes, until the carrots soften.
3. Add the rice and crumbled tofu. Season with 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tbsp cornstarch, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1/2 tsp sugar, the rice vinegar, and the white pepper. Mix well, taste, and add salt or pepper as needed. Let cool.
4. Gently peel off the cabbage leaves without tearing them. Blanch in boiling water for 20–30 seconds, just until they start to wilt, then shock in cold water.
5. Put about 2 tbsp of filling on the lower half of each leaf and roll up like a spring roll, tucking in the sides.
6. Pan-fry the rolls seam-side down for 2–3 minutes until lightly seared, then flip and sear the other side for 2–3 minutes.
7. For the sauce, bring the remaining 1 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tsp sugar, 1 tsp sesame oil, and 1/2 cup water to a boil, then lower to medium-low. Stir in 1 tsp cornstarch dissolved in 2 tsp cold water and simmer until thickened. Pour over the rolls.

## Notes

- Swap in any grain for the rice, leave the rice out for a lower-carb version, or use another ground protein instead of tofu.
