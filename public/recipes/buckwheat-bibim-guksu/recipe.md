## Ingredients

- Buckwheat soba noodles, for 2
- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 1 1/2 tbsp sugar
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- Sesame seeds
- Cucumber, cut into matchsticks
- Soft-boiled eggs, halved

## Instructions

1. Whisk the gochujang, sugar, rice vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, and sesame seeds in a big bowl.
2. Cook the noodles, then rinse under cold water and drain well.
3. Toss the noodles in the sauce.
4. Serve topped with cucumber, a soft-boiled egg, and more sesame seeds.
