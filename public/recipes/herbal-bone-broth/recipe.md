## Ingredients

- Chicken carcass
- Onions
- Garlic
- Celery
- Eleuthero root
- Astragalus root
- Burdock root
- Ginger root
- Reishi mushrooms
- A little apple cider vinegar
- Water

## Instructions

1. Put the chicken carcass, onions, garlic, and celery in an Instant Pot.
2. Add the eleuthero root, astragalus root, burdock root, ginger root, and reishi mushrooms.
3. Add a little apple cider vinegar to help extract the herbs, and cover with water.
4. Pressure cook for 2 hours.
5. Strain. Drink it on its own or use it in soups and chilis.
