## Ingredients

- 1/2 pound (225 grams) pasta, such as ziti or a twisty shape
- 1 tablespoon (15 ml) olive oil
- 3/4 pound (340 grams) fresh mushrooms, sliced (pre-sliced cremini work well)
- 1 small-to-medium yellow onion, halved and sliced thin
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1/4 cup (60 ml) dry marsala wine (see notes)
- 3 tablespoons (45 grams) unsalted butter
- 3 tablespoons (25 grams) all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 cups (355 ml) stock or broth (chicken, vegetable or mushroom)
- 1/2 cup (50 grams) finely grated parmesan cheese
- 4 ounces (115 grams) mozzarella, cut into small cubes
- 3 tablespoons chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley

## Instructions

1. Cook the pasta: Bring a pot of well-salted water to a boil. Add pasta and cook until 1 to 2 minutes before al dente. Drain and set aside.
2. Heat oven to 400°F.
3. Make the sauce: Reheat the empty pasta pot (or a 3-to-4 quart stovetop-to-oven dish) over high heat. Add oil and, once hot, add mushrooms and cook until they've begun to brown and glisten, but have not yet released their liquid. Reduce heat to medium-high, add onions, salt and pepper and sauté together until the liquid the mushrooms give off has evaporated.
4. Add Marsala and cook, stirring, until it has almost or fully evaporated. Add butter, stir until melted. Add flour, and stir until all of it has been dampened and absorbed.
5. Add stock a very small splash at a time, stirring constantly and scraping the bottom and sides, making sure each splash is fully mixed in before adding the next. Once all the stock is in, simmer 2 minutes, stirring frequently; the sauce will thicken. Remove from heat.
6. Assemble: If your pan is ovensafe, add the cooked pasta and stir until combined (otherwise transfer everything to a 2-quart baking dish). Stir in half the parmesan, all of the mozzarella and two tablespoons of the parsley. Sprinkle the top with the remaining parmesan.
7. Bake 25 to 30 minutes, until the edges of the pasta are golden brown. Sprinkle with the reserved parsley and serve hot.

## Notes

- Serves 4 generously or 6 more moderately. For a crowd, double it in a 9×13-inch pan.
- Use dry (not sweet) Marsala; Sherry or Madeira would also taste good. It keeps opened in the fridge a long time.
- Without alcohol: rehydrate a few dried porcini in 1/4 cup boiling water, chop them and add to the mushrooms, then strain the soaking liquid, add 1 teaspoon sherry or red wine vinegar, and use it instead of the Marsala.
- Diced cooked chicken can be added before baking.
