## Ingredients

- Fusilli
- 5 sausages, casings removed
- Olive oil
- 1/2 stick butter
- 1 small white onion, diced
- 3–4 cloves garlic, sliced
- 2 tsp chili flakes
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 oz vodka
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 2 cups marinara sauce
- 2 tbsp salt
- 1 tbsp pepper
- 1 bunch basil, leaves only
- 1 cup grated Parmesan, plus more to serve

## Instructions

1. Boil the pasta according to the package directions, then drain and set aside.
2. Break the sausage into small pieces and sear in olive oil until golden brown. Remove from the pan.
3. Add the butter and sweat the onion, sliced garlic, and chili flakes until fully cooked.
4. Add the tomato paste and cook 2 minutes.
5. Deglaze with the vodka, scraping up all the bits from the bottom of the pan.
6. Add the cream and marinara and bring to a simmer.
7. Once the sauce starts to thicken, return the sausage and season with the salt and pepper.
8. When the sauce is the consistency you like, stir in the pasta, Parmesan, and basil until everything is combined.
9. Serve with more Parmesan and basil.
