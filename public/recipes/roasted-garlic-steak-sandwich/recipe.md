## Ingredients

- Head of garlic
- Olive oil
- Butter
- Fresh rosemary and thyme
- Crusty bread
- Steak
- Onions, sliced
- Broth and a dark sauce, such as Worcestershire or soy, for the jus
- Boursin garlic and herb cheese
- Melting cheese, sliced, and grated cheese
- Arugula

## Instructions

1. Roast a head of garlic wrapped in foil with olive oil, then mash the cloves into softened butter with chopped herbs.
2. Spread the garlic butter on the cut sides of the bread and toast.
3. Sear the steak in a hot pan, basting with butter, rosemary, and thyme. Rest and slice.
4. Cook the onions in the same pan until caramelized, then add broth and the dark sauce to make a jus.
5. Spread Boursin on the bread, layer the steak, cheese, and onions, add arugula, and serve with the jus for dipping.
