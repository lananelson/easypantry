## Ingredients

- 1 good-quality tuna steak, line-caught
- 1 head butter lettuce
- 8 stems tenderstem broccoli
- 200 g peas
- 2 shallots
- 4 eggs
- 2 tbsp olive oil, plus more for the tuna
- 1 tbsp freshly squeezed lemon juice
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp wholegrain mustard
- Salt and pepper
- 6 oil-packed anchovies, chopped, plus a few tbsp of their oil
- 10 black olives, pitted

## Instructions

1. Boil the eggs for about 8 minutes, then move them to an ice bath. Steam the broccoli for 4 minutes (or boil it if you don't have a steamer). Boil the peas in well-salted water for 2 minutes. Drain the vegetables and set aside.
2. Finely chop the shallots and put them in a large bowl. Add the lemon juice, 2 tbsp olive oil, honey, mustard, and a good pinch of salt and cracked black pepper. Mix, then add the broccoli and peas.
3. Tear in the lettuce, add the chopped anchovies and a drizzle of their oil, then the olives, and toss together. Peel the eggs so they're ready to go.
4. Rub the tuna steak with a drizzle of olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Sear in a very hot pan for 2 minutes a side at most, then slice and toss into the salad.
