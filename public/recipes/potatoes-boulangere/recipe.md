## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 pounds potatoes
- 2 small onions (or 1 fairly large)
- 1/3 cup vegetable (or chicken) broth, steaming hot
- 1/3 cup milk
- 1 or 2 oz butter
- pepper
- salt
- Optional: garlic powder & dried parsley (or a garlic clove to rub the dish)

## Instructions

1. Peel and thinly slice the potatoes (a mandolin is fine). Rinse the slices to remove the surface starch and dab them dry with a tea towel.
2. Thinly slice the onions.
3. Layer potatoes and onions in a baking dish, as many layers as you like, seasoning each layer generously with salt and black pepper. Traditionally you end with potatoes so they get crunchy, but ending with onions works too.
4. Combine the hot broth with the milk, plus garlic powder and a touch of dried parsley if using. (Instead of garlic powder, you can rub the dish with a halved garlic clove.)
5. Pour the hot broth mix over the potatoes and top with tiny pieces of butter, about 1 oz (or 2 if you like them buttery). Cover with aluminum foil and bake in a preheated 350°F (180°C) oven for 40 minutes.
6. Remove the foil and bake uncovered for another 35 to 40 minutes, until the top layer is slightly golden brown and crispy and the potatoes are done.

## Notes

- Classic with a juicy steak and greens on the side.
