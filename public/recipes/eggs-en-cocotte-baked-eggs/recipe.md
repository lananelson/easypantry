## Ingredients

- 4 whole eggs
- 4 tbsp heavy cream
- 4 tbsp Boursin cheese
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp chopped chives
- Salt and freshly cracked pepper
- 2 slices grainy, seedy bread

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and bring a kettle of water to a boil. Butter each ramekin lightly. Crack two eggs into each ramekin, then top each egg with cream and crumbled cheese.
2. Put the ramekins in a high-sided baking dish and pour in boiling water to come halfway up the sides of the ramekins. Cover the ramekins with their lids, or cover the whole baking dish with foil.
3. Bake for 12–25 minutes, until the eggs are done to your liking. Season with salt and pepper and serve with chopped chives and buttery toast.

## Notes

- Check the eggs at 12 minutes: the cream should be just barely bubbling at the sides, with a slight wobble on top when you gently shake a ramekin. If they're not there yet, check again every 2 minutes.
- Boursin is a soft French cheese seasoned with garlic and herbs; herbed goat cheese is a good substitute.
- Variations: mushroom and Gruyère; spinach and goat cheese; sun-dried tomato and feta; bacon and Cheddar; black pepper and Parmesan.
