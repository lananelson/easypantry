## Ingredients

- 1 medium head radicchio (about 8 ounces)
- 3 garlic cloves, unpeeled
- 2 ounces Piave, Parmesan, or pecorino cheese
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 2 large bone-in, skin-on chicken breasts (about 2 pounds)
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 5 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 cup Castelvetrano olives
- 1/4 baguette (about 3 ounces)
- 3 oil-packed anchovy fillets
- 3 tablespoons sherry vinegar
- Honey, for drizzling

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
2. Pat the chicken dry and season it evenly all over with 2 1/2 teaspoons salt and lots of black pepper.
3. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a 12-inch cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat until just beginning to smoke. Place the chicken breasts skin-side down in the skillet and reduce the heat to medium. Cook, undisturbed, until the skin is golden brown, 6 to 8 minutes. Move the breasts around the pan if the chicken seems to be browning more quickly in some parts than others. Transfer to the oven, still skin-side down, and roast until an instant-read thermometer registers 145°F when inserted into the thickest part of the breast, 25 to 30 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, tear the radicchio into large pieces and transfer to a large bowl. Using a vegetable peeler, shave the cheese into the radicchio. Smash the Castelvetrano olives with the bottom of a mug or glass measuring cup, discard the pits, and add the olives to the salad. Tear the baguette into irregular 1-inch pieces and set aside.
5. Smash the garlic cloves but don't peel them. When the chicken is done, remove the skillet from the oven and transfer it to the stovetop; keep the oven on. Flip the chicken breasts skin-side up. Add the butter, garlic, and anchovy fillets to the skillet and set over medium heat. Tilt the pan slightly toward you, let the melted butter collect at the side of the pan, and continuously baste the chicken breasts with the butter for 2 minutes. Transfer the chicken to a cutting board to rest, leaving the anchovy-garlic butter behind. Add the torn bread to the butter and stir the pieces around to coat. Return the skillet to the oven and bake until the croutons are crisp and golden brown, 8 to 10 minutes.
6. Add the croutons and garlic to the bowl of salad. Drizzle 3 tablespoons sherry vinegar and 3 tablespoons olive oil over the radicchio; toss well. Season with salt and black pepper. Lightly drizzle the salad with honey, tossing once to create pockets of sweetness.
7. Carve the chicken breasts off the bone, slice them crosswise, and serve with the salad.

## Adaptations

- **Rotisserie chicken:** Remove the breasts from a rotisserie chicken. Make the anchovy-garlic butter as written, then briefly sear and warm the breasts in it, spooning the butter over them, just until heated through and lightly crisped. Continue with the bread and salad as written.

## Notes

- Molly notes that chicken breasts are easier to overcook than legs or thighs. Keeping both the skin and bone intact protects the meat while roasting: the skin acts as a barrier and the bone contributes flavor. The final butter basting helps keep the breast moist.
- Leave the smashed garlic cloves unpeeled while basting; the skins protect the garlic from burning.
