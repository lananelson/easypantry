## Ingredients

- 1/2 lb bucatini
- 1/4 cup + 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, plus more to finish
- 1/2 bulb fennel, thinly sliced, fronds saved
- 1/2 medium yellow onion, finely diced
- Red pepper flakes
- Salt and black pepper
- 1/2 cup plain breadcrumbs
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- Anchovies
- 1 can skinless, boneless sardines in olive oil
- 1/4 cup white wine
- 2 tbsp pine nuts
- 2 tbsp currants
- Fresh parsley, chopped

## Instructions

1. Heat 1/4 cup olive oil in a sauté pan. Add the fennel, onion, red pepper flakes, black pepper, and a little salt and cook until soft.
2. In a small pan, toast the breadcrumbs in 2 tbsp olive oil with salt and pepper until golden and crisp.
3. Add the garlic to the fennel and onion, then the white wine.
4. Add the anchovies and sardines and break them up in the oil. Add the pine nuts and currants and cook about 10 minutes, until it all comes together. Stir in some parsley.
5. Cook the bucatini and drain it straight into the pan. Toss with a splash of pasta water and more parsley.
6. Top with the toasted breadcrumbs, fennel fronds, and a drizzle of olive oil.

## Notes

- Double everything for a full pound of pasta, to feed four.
- A little tomato sauce can be stirred in, though it's usually made without.
