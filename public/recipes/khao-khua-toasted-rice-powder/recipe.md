## Ingredients

- Glutinous (sticky) rice, uncooked
- Lemongrass, sliced
- Makrut lime leaves

## Instructions

1. Toast the rice and lemongrass in a dry pan over medium-low heat, stirring to spread the lemongrass through the rice.
2. Let it sit a few minutes, then stir almost constantly until the rice is deep golden and fragrant.
3. Add the lime leaves and keep toasting briefly.
4. Pound in a mortar and pestle a spoonful at a time into a coarse powder.

## Notes

- This is what makes laab taste like laab; see [Crispy Tofu Laab](../crispy-tofu-laab/recipe.md).
