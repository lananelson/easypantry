## Ingredients

- Oil
- Green onions, diced
- Bacon, Spam, or other pork (or mushrooms)
- Kimchi
- Sugar
- Soy sauce
- Gochujang
- Cooked rice
- Sesame oil
- Fried eggs, nori, or cheese, to top

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a frying pan and cook the green onions to make green onion oil.
2. Add the bacon (or Spam, pork, or mushrooms) and cook, then add the kimchi and keep sautéing.
3. Stir in the sugar, soy sauce, and gochujang.
4. Add the rice and a drizzle of sesame oil and mix until well combined.
5. Top with a fried egg, nori, or cheese.
