## Ingredients

- 1 to 1 1/2 pounds broccoli rabe
- At least 4 quarts water
- 1 tablespoon coarse sea salt
- 1 pound orecchiette
- 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 3 large garlic cloves, minced
- 6 anchovy fillets under oil, drained and minced; or 2 to 3 anchovies under salt, rinsed and minced
- 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes or 1 fresh hot red pepper, minced
- Salt
- 3 to 4 tablespoons best-quality extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 to 3 ounces ricotta salata cheese

## Instructions

1. Wash the broccoli rabe well. Discard thick stalks, peel the thinner stems and cut them into 2-inch pieces. Roughly chop the tender sprouts and leaves.
2. Bring at least 4 quarts water to a rolling boil. Add the coarse salt, then the pasta. Check the package cooking time; orecchiette may take 10 to 18 minutes. Add the broccoli rabe when 6 to 7 minutes remain so the pasta and greens finish together.
3. Meanwhile, warm 3 tablespoons olive oil over low-medium heat in a medium sauté pan. Add the garlic and anchovies, mash them with a wooden spoon to a paste, and cook 4 to 5 minutes, until the garlic is barely softened and beginning to turn golden.
4. Add the red pepper flakes and cook briefly. Taste for salt.
5. Drain the pasta and broccoli rabe and toss them in the pan with the anchovies and garlic.
6. Transfer to a heated serving platter, toss with the best-quality extra-virgin olive oil, grate the ricotta salata over the top, and serve immediately.

## Notes

- The source says dried orecchiette is fine; homemade is not required.
- If broccoli rabe is unavailable, the source suggests dandelion greens for a similarly bitter character, or broccoli for a subtler taste.
