## Ingredients

- 2 cups extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 onions
- 1 large carrot
- 12 garlic cloves
- 1 (10-pound) can peeled tomatoes
- 1 tablespoon crushed red chile flakes
- 2.25 ounces (1/2 of a 4.5-ounce tube) tomato paste
- 1 tablespoon kosher salt
- 2 tablespoons freshly ground black pepper
- 1 teaspoon to 1 tablespoon sugar

## Instructions

1. In a heavy-bottomed pot, heat the olive oil over medium heat.
2. Using the largest holes of a box grater, grate the onions directly into the pot.
3. Grate the carrot the same way, then roughly hand-chop the grated carrot and add it to the pot.
4. Finely chop the garlic and add it.
5. Turn the heat to low and cook very slowly for 4 hours.
6. Run the peeled tomatoes through a food mill or mashed potato ricer directly into the pot with the soffritto. If you do not have a food mill or ricer, empty the tomatoes into a bowl and break them up well by hand.
7. Add the chile flakes, tomato paste, salt, black pepper, and sugar, adjusting the sugar based on the sweetness of the tomatoes.
8. Cook uncovered over low heat for 4 more hours, stirring every 5 to 10 minutes, until the sauce is deeper and richer in color and the fat is visibly separating from the sauce.

## Notes

- For a quicker version, Christian Petroni suggests halving the recipe, cooking the soffritto more quickly, and simmering the sauce for about 30 minutes over higher heat while stirring frequently.
- The book notes that the sauce keeps for up to 2 weeks in the refrigerator and 2 months in the freezer.
