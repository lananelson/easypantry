## Ingredients

- 4 lb bone-in short ribs
- Salt and pepper
- White onion, celery, and carrot (2 parts onion, 1 part each celery and carrot)
- A few cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 cups red wine
- 3 cups beef broth
- Parsley, thyme, rosemary, and bay leaves
- Dijon mustard
- 2 tbsp cold butter
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar (optional)
- Chives, minced
- Mashed potatoes or polenta, to serve

## Instructions

1. Season the short ribs with salt and pepper on all sides and sear over high heat until browned all over. Remove.
2. Cook the onion, celery, and carrot in the drippings, then add the garlic. After a couple of minutes, add the tomato paste and let it cook.
3. Add the red wine and broth and bring to a simmer. Add the herbs.
4. Brush the short ribs with Dijon and return them to the pot; the liquid should cover at least 75% of the meat.
5. Cover and braise in a 300°F oven at least 3 hours, until fall-off-the-bone tender.
6. Boil about 2 cups of the braising liquid, reduce to a simmer, and swirl in the cold butter (and balsamic, if using) until glossy.
7. Serve on mashed potatoes or polenta, topped with the sauce and chives.
