## Ingredients

- 8 eggs
- ½ cup Greek yogurt
- 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- 1 bunch radishes
- 1 bunch chives
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt
- Sprouts
- 8 slices whole grain bread

## Instructions

1. Hard boil the eggs (A Couple Cooks' method: http://www.acouplecooks.com/2011/04/perfect-hard-boiled-eggs/).
2. When the eggs are done and cooled, peel them, then chop them roughly.
3. Wash the radishes, then cut them in half and thinly slice them. Thinly slice enough chives for 3 tablespoons.
4. In a bowl, combine the eggs, radishes, and chives with the Greek yogurt, Dijon mustard, and kosher salt. Taste, then add more seasonings as desired.
5. To assemble, spread egg salad on one bread slice, then top with sprouts and another bread slice.
