## Ingredients

- 1 lb elbow macaroni
- Salt
- Sharp cheddar, mild cheddar, and Colby Jack (part shredded, part cubed)
- 1 egg per pound of pasta
- Seasoning salt
- Brown mustard
- Nature's Seasons seasoning
- Evaporated milk
- Whole milk
- 1/2 stick butter
- Sour cream
- A little Velveeta

## Instructions

1. Shred some of the cheese into long shreds and cube the rest.
2. Boil the macaroni in well-salted water until al dente, then drain into a big bowl.
3. Whisk the egg with seasoning salt, brown mustard, and Nature's Seasons, then whisk in evaporated milk and whole milk to make a custard.
4. Stir the butter into the hot pasta until melted, then a spoonful of sour cream and some of the cheese.
5. Add the custard, the rest of the cubed cheese, and a little Velveeta, and mix until creamy.
6. Spread in an ungreased baking dish and top with shredded cheese.
7. Cover loosely with foil and bake at 375°F about 20 minutes, then uncover and bake 10–15 minutes more until browned.
