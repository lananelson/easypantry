## Ingredients

- Olive oil
- 1 onion, peeled and sliced
- 2 (400 g) tins butter beans
- 1 (400 g) tin full-fat coconut milk
- 1 tbsp white miso paste
- Salt and pepper
- 100 g baby spinach
- 1 handful fresh dill
- ½ lemon, juice only
- 6 slices toasted bread, to serve (or gluten-free bread)

## Instructions

1. Drizzle a splash of olive oil into a large pan or pot over low-medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 6–8 minutes, until translucent (don't let it brown).
2. Add the butter beans, including all the juices from the tins. Turn the heat up and cook for 7–10 minutes, until most of the liquid has reduced.
3. Scoop out the coconut cream that has set at the top of the tin of coconut milk and add most of it to the pan, reserving a few dollops for topping. Stir in the miso paste and a generous pinch of pepper, then cook for a minute.
4. The sauce should be creamy and slightly runny; if it's too thin, keep cooking until it thickens slightly. Stir in the spinach and cook for another minute, until wilted.
5. Chop most of the dill, saving a few leaves for topping, and add it to the pan with the lemon juice. Stir well, taste, and season with salt and more pepper if needed.
6. Remove from the heat and top with the remaining dill leaves, a splash of olive oil, freshly ground black pepper, and dollops of the reserved coconut cream. Serve with toasted bread for dunking.

## Notes

- Serve with toasted bread, or with boiled potatoes and vegetables for a larger meal.
- Save the leftover coconut water from the tin for smoothies.
