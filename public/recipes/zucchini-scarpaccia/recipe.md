## Ingredients

- 3 zucchini
- 1 onion
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp herbs, such as basil, oregano, or thyme
- 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup cornmeal
- 2 tbsp olive oil

## Instructions

1. Thinly slice the zucchini and onion with a mandoline. Toss them in a colander with the salt and let sit about 15 minutes.
2. Squeeze the zucchini and onion to remove the excess liquid, saving the liquid.
3. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
4. Mix the flour, cornmeal, nutritional yeast, garlic, and herbs. Gradually stir in 1 cup of the saved liquid to make a smooth batter, adding a little water if needed.
5. Fold in the squeezed zucchini and onion.
6. Grease a baking dish or tray with the olive oil and spread the mixture in evenly.
7. Bake 30–35 minutes, until golden and firm. Cool a few minutes, then cut into squares or wedges.

## Notes

- A good way to use up wilted vegetables.
