## Ingredients

- 8 garlic cloves
- 1 large white onion
- 1 bunch broccoli rabe (about 1 pound)
- 1 bunch kale, Swiss chard, spinach, or mustard greens (about 1 pound)
- 8 ounces sharp provolone, cheddar, or Gruyère cheese
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 1/4 cup plus 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 cup panko bread crumbs
- Kosher salt
- 3 (14.5-ounce) cans butter beans, gigante beans, or other white beans
- 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 450°F.
2. Heat 3 tablespoons olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat. Add 1 cup panko and season with 1/2 teaspoon salt. Cook, stirring frequently, until the panko is light golden brown, 4 to 5 minutes. Transfer the panko to a bowl to cool. Wipe out the Dutch oven.
3. Grate 8 ounces sharp provolone cheese (about 2 cups) and transfer to a medium bowl. Firmly smash and peel 8 garlic cloves, then roughly chop them. Cut the white onion in half through the root end, peel and discard the skin, then thinly slice through the root end. Drain and rinse the beans in a fine-mesh strainer.
4. Heat 1/4 cup olive oil in the Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the onions and garlic and cook, stirring occasionally, until the onions are soft and beginning to brown at the edges, 10 to 12 minutes.
5. Trim off the bottom inch of the broccoli rabe, then chop the bunch crosswise into roughly 2-inch pieces. Strip the leaves from the kale and tear them into 3- to 4-inch pieces.
6. Add the broccoli rabe to the onions and cook, tossing often with tongs, until it begins to cook down and wilt, about 1 minute. Add the kale, 1 tablespoon salt, and 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes. Cook, stirring, until the kale and broccoli rabe turn bright green and lose some of their volume, 3 to 5 minutes. Remove from the heat.
7. Stir in 2 cups heavy cream and the drained beans. Working one handful at a time, stir in all but one large handful of the grated cheese; it will not fully melt at this point.
8. Scatter the reserved handful of cheese over the top, followed by the toasted panko. Transfer the Dutch oven to the oven and bake until bubbling and thick with a golden brown crust around the edges, 20 to 25 minutes. Let sit 5 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- Molly describes this as essentially mac and cheese in disguise, with beans standing in for pasta and soaking up the creamy sauce as it bakes.
- Broccolini or broccoli can stand in for the broccoli rabe, or the kale can be doubled.
- Toast the panko fully before baking; the breadcrumbs will not brown much more in the oven because the moisture in the dish inhibits browning.
