## Ingredients

- Bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- Salt and pepper
- Ground coriander
- Oil
- Shallot, finely chopped
- White wine
- Chicken stock
- Crème fraîche (or cream)
- Chives, parsley, and tarragon, finely chopped
- Fennel fronds (instead of hard-to-find chervil)
- Shaved fennel and Belgian endive, for the salad

## Instructions

1. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, and ground coriander. Sear skin side down in an ovenproof pan until crisp, then finish in the oven.
2. While it cooks, chop the shallot and herbs, and shave the fennel and endive for the salad.
3. Pour off most of the fat, keeping the browned bits. Sweat the shallot in the pan.
4. Deglaze with white wine and reduce by about three-quarters. Add chicken stock and reduce by half.
5. Stir in crème fraîche and cook to a thick but pourable sauce, then stir in the herbs.
6. Serve the chicken with the sauce and the fennel salad.
