## Ingredients

- 2 sticks butter
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/2 cup water
- Salt
- Nutmeg
- Cayenne pepper
- 1 cup flour
- 5 eggs
- 1 big spoonful Dijon mustard
- About 1 1/2 cups grated Gruyère (or sharp white cheddar)
- Parmesan, for sprinkling

## Instructions

1. Bring the butter, milk, water, salt, nutmeg, and cayenne to a boil in a pot.
2. Add the flour and stir over medium heat until it forms a dough. Keep cooking until most of the moisture is gone and the dough starts to dry out.
3. Transfer to a mixer and beat in the eggs one at a time. Add the Dijon and the cheese.
4. Taste the dough and add more salt, cayenne, or nutmeg as needed.
5. Scoop tablespoon portions onto parchment-lined sheets and sprinkle with Parmesan.
6. Put in a 425°F oven and immediately lower to 375°F. Bake 12 minutes, rotate the sheets, and bake 12–15 minutes more, until deep golden brown.
