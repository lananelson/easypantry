## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons (30ml) extra-virgin olive oil (lentils)
- 2 small carrots, peeled and cut into 1/2-inch chunks, about 1 cup (lentils)
- 2 small stalks celery, cut into 1/4-inch slices, about 1 cup (lentils)
- 1 medium onion, finely diced, about 1 cup (lentils)
- 6 medium cloves garlic, thinly sliced (lentils)
- 12 ounces (340g) brown or de Puy lentils (lentils)
- 2 bay leaves (lentils)
- 4 cups homemade vegetable stock or water, about 1L (lentils)
- Kosher salt (lentils)
- 2 teaspoons (10ml) red wine vinegar, apple cider vinegar, or sherry vinegar (lentils)
- Freshly ground black pepper (lentils)
- 2 large Italian or small globe eggplants, about 1 pound (450g) each (eggplant)
- 4 tablespoons (60ml) extra-virgin olive oil (eggplant)
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper (eggplant)
- 4 large sprigs fresh rosemary (eggplant)
- 1 whole head garlic, broken into individual unpeeled cloves, about 20 (tahini sauce)
- 2/3 cup fresh juice from 3 to 4 lemons, 160ml (tahini sauce)
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin (tahini sauce)
- 1 generous cup tahini paste, about 300g (tahini sauce)
- 2 tablespoons (30ml) extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for drizzling (to serve)
- 1/4 cup pine nuts (to serve)
- 2 tablespoons minced fresh parsley leaves (to serve)
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh rosemary leaves (to serve)

## Instructions

1. Tahini sauce: Combine garlic and lemon juice in a blender. Pulse until a pulpy puree is formed, about 15 short pulses. Transfer to a fine mesh strainer set over a large bowl. Press out as much liquid as you can with the back of a spoon or a rubber spatula, then discard solids.
2. Add cumin and tahini to the lemon/garlic juice and whisk to combine. The mixture will seize up and turn pasty. Add water a few tablespoons at a time, whisking in between each addition, until a smooth, light sauce forms. It should very slowly lose its shape if you let ribbons drop from the whisk. Season to taste with salt. Refrigerates up to 1 1/2 weeks.
3. Lentils: Adjust oven rack to center position and preheat oven to 450°F for the eggplant. Meanwhile, heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat until shimmering. Add carrots, celery, and onion and cook, stirring, until softened but not browned, about 4 minutes. Add garlic and cook, stirring, until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add lentils, bay leaves, stock or water, and a pinch of salt. Bring to a simmer, cover with the lid partially ajar, and cook until lentils are tender, about 30 minutes. (Top up with water if lentils are at any point not fully submerged.) Remove lid, stir in vinegar, and reduce until lentils are moist but not soupy. Season to taste with salt and pepper, cover, and keep warm until ready to serve.
4. Eggplant: While lentils cook, cut each eggplant in half. Score flesh with the tip of a paring knife in a cross-hatch pattern at 1-inch intervals. Transfer to a foil-lined rimmed baking sheet, cut side up, and brush each half with 1 tablespoon oil, letting each brushstroke be fully absorbed before brushing with more. Season with salt and pepper. Place a rosemary sprig on top of each one. Roast until completely tender and well charred, 25 to 35 minutes. Discard rosemary.
5. To serve: Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil and pine nuts in a medium skillet over medium heat. Cook, tossing frequently, until golden brown and aromatic, about 4 minutes. Transfer to a bowl to halt cooking. Stir half of the parsley and rosemary into the lentils and transfer to a serving platter. Arrange eggplant halves on top. Spread a few tablespoons of tahini sauce over each eggplant half and sprinkle with pine nuts. Sprinkle with remaining parsley and rosemary, drizzle with additional olive oil, and serve.
