## Ingredients

- Firm tofu, crumbled
- Canned tuna
- Cabbage, shredded
- Perilla leaves, some sliced, some whole
- Green onions, sliced
- Egg
- Starch
- Black pepper and salt
- Rice paper
- Oil, for frying

## Instructions

1. Mix the tofu, tuna, cabbage, sliced perilla, green onions, egg, starch, pepper, and salt into a filling.
2. Dip a sheet of rice paper in water, lay a whole perilla leaf on it, add filling, and fold into a packet.
3. Pan-fry in oil until crisp and golden on both sides.
