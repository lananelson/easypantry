## Ingredients

- 1 or 2 cucumbers (depending on size)
- 1 avocado
- 1/2 small red onion
- 2 Tbsp hemp seeds
- pinch of cayenne
- 1-2 Tbsp coconut aminos (or Bragg's Aminos or tamari)
- Optional: grape tomatoes, seaweed (dulse, hijiki, arame)

## Instructions

1. Cut cucumber, avocado, and onion into bite sized pieces. Put into a bowl.
2. Sprinkle hemp seeds and a dash of cayenne (to taste) on top.
3. Add coconut aminos to taste and gently mix well.
4. Let marinate in the fridge for at least an hour so the flavors meld.
