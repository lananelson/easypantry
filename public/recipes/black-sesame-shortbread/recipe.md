## Ingredients

- 10 tablespoons (1 1/4 sticks / 142 g) unsalted butter
- 1 1/2 cups (180 g) all-purpose flour
- 2/3 cup (75 g) powdered sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons (4 g) kosher salt
- 1/4 cup (35 g) black or white sesame seeds
- 1/3 cup (80 g) tahini
- Granulated sugar, for sprinkling

## Instructions

1. Position a rack in the center of the oven. Preheat the oven to 325°F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Cut the butter into 1-inch pieces. Melt it in a small saucepan over medium heat, swirling occasionally, about 3 minutes. Set aside to cool.
3. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, powdered sugar, and kosher salt.
4. Place the sesame seeds in a small resealable plastic bag, seal it, and crush them by smashing lightly with the side of a wine bottle or rolling pin. Alternatively, use a mortar and pestle or spice grinder. Add the crushed sesame seeds to the dry ingredients and whisk to combine.
5. Whisk the tahini into the cooled melted butter, then pour the mixture into the dry ingredients, stirring with a spatula until a dough comes together in a rough ball. Using your hands, knead the dough lightly a few times until no floury spots remain.
6. Transfer the dough to the prepared baking sheet and use your hands to pat it into a 1/2-inch-thick, 8 x 8-inch square. Smooth the surface with the bottom of a measuring cup so it is flat.
7. Use a fork to lightly prick the dough all over. Transfer the baking sheet to the refrigerator and chill for at least 30 minutes or up to overnight. If chilling overnight, lightly cover the dough with plastic wrap.
8. Once chilled, cut the shortbread into 16 equal 2-inch squares. Space them out evenly on the baking sheet.
9. Bake until lightly golden brown all over, 30 to 40 minutes. While still warm, lightly sprinkle granulated sugar over the surface, just enough to evenly coat each square. Let the cookies cool completely on the baking sheet before serving.

## Notes

- Molly describes this as a melt-in-your-mouth shortbread that avoids the usual butter-creaming technique: melted butter and tahini are stirred directly into the dry ingredients.
- Powdered sugar contains cornstarch, which helps give the shortbread a fine, delicate texture without creaming the butter.
- Crushing the sesame seeds releases some of their oils and helps flavor the dough.
- Pricking the dough lets steam escape so the cookies bake evenly and stay crisp.
