## Ingredients

- Mac and cheese (stovetop, made the day before)
- Flour
- Eggs, beaten
- Flamin' Hot Cheetos, crushed
- Oil, for frying
- Ranch or blue cheese dressing, to serve

## Instructions

1. Make the mac and cheese and refrigerate overnight until firm.
2. Scoop into balls.
3. Coat each in flour, then egg wash, then crushed Cheetos.
4. Fry until crisp on all sides.
5. Serve with ranch or blue cheese dressing.
