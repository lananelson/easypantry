## Ingredients

- 2 portobello mushrooms
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes
- 1 bunch arugula
- 2 roasted red peppers
- 3 mozzarella balls
- 1/2 yellow onion
- 4 cloves of garlic
- Balsamic vinegar
- 1/2 package farfalle

## Instructions

1. Drizzle vinegar over sliced mushrooms, mix well.
2. Sauté onions in oil over medium high heat until lightly browned. Add mushrooms and sauté until soft and juicy. Add smashed garlic and sauté for 3 min. Turn off the heat.
3. Meanwhile prepare pasta al dente and rinse with cold water.
4. Add pasta and the rest of the ingredients, cut to bite size pieces, to the mushroom mix.
