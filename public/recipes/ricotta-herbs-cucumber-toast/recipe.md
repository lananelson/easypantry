## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup low-fat ricotta cheese
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh basil, parsley, or dill
- Salt and pepper
- Lemon juice
- Toast
- Cucumber, sliced

## Instructions

1. Stir the herbs into the ricotta. Season with salt, pepper, and lemon juice.
2. Spread on toast; top with cucumber.
