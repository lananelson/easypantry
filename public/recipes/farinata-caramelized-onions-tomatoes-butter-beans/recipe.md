## Ingredients

### Farinata base

- 8 ounces (2 cups / 226 g) finely ground chickpea flour
- 1 teaspoon Diamond Crystal kosher salt
- 710 ml water
- 60 ml extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1 teaspoon rosemary

### Topping

- Jarred butter beans, drained
- Roma tomatoes
- Anchovies
- Fresh rosemary
- Fresh thyme
- Garlic
- Red onions
- Balsamic vinegar
- Fresh basil
- Parmesan or pecorino

## Instructions

1. In a mixing bowl, combine the chickpea flour and salt. Gradually whisk in the water until smooth and thin. Cover and let rest for at least 4 hours and up to 8 hours.
2. Preheat the oven to 550°F with a rack in the second position from the top. If using a pizza stone or baking steel, preheat it on the rack.
3. Pour the olive oil into a well-seasoned 12-inch cast-iron skillet and swirl to coat the bottom. Skim any foam from the farinata batter, stir it well, and pour it into the skillet. Season with black pepper and rosemary.
4. Turn on the broiler and cook until the farinata is set, no longer jiggles, and is browned on top, about 11 to 15 minutes.
5. Let cool slightly, about 10 minutes.
6. For the topping, caramelize the red onions with balsamic vinegar.
7. Cook the Roma tomatoes and butter beans with anchovies, rosemary, thyme, and garlic until hot and well seasoned.
8. Spoon the tomato-butter-bean mixture over the farinata, add the caramelized onions, and finish with fresh basil and grated Parmesan or pecorino.
