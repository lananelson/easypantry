## Ingredients

- 4–5 russet potatoes
- 10 cloves garlic, smashed
- Salt and pepper
- High-smoke-point oil
- 4 tbsp butter
- Fresh rosemary
- Fresh thyme
- 1/2 cup chicken stock

## Instructions

1. Peel the potatoes and slice into 1-inch rounds. Cut into circles with a ring cutter, or into squares with a knife.
2. Soak in water for 20 minutes to draw out the starch, then pat dry and season all over with salt and pepper.
3. Heat high-smoke-point oil in an ovenproof pan and sear the potatoes 3–4 minutes without moving them, until a good crust forms. Flip.
4. Turn the heat down to low and add the butter, rosemary, thyme, and garlic.
5. Pour in the chicken stock so the potatoes are partly submerged and bring to a simmer.
6. Transfer to a 400°F oven for 15–20 minutes, until fork-tender.
