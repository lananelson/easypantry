## Ingredients

- Mackerel fillets
- Olive oil
- Salt
- Fennel, thinly shaved
- Orange
- Fresh mint
- Fresh dill
- Lemon

## Instructions

1. Brush the mackerel with olive oil, salt it, and grill skin side down until crisp and cooked through.
2. Peel and segment the orange.
3. Toss the shaved fennel with the orange, chopped mint and dill, a squeeze of lemon, olive oil, and salt.
4. Serve the mackerel topped with the salad.
