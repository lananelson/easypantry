## Ingredients

- 1 cup dry millet
- 3 cups water or light chicken stock (for the millet)
- 1 cup fava beans
- 1 cup shelled green peas
- 16 green long asparagus stems
- Sea salt and pepper (vinaigrette)
- 1 teaspoon honey-flavored mustard (vinaigrette)
- Juice of 1 large lemon (vinaigrette)
- 1 garlic clove, peeled and finely minced (vinaigrette)
- 3 tablespoons olive oil (vinaigrette)
- 1 tablespoon pistachio oil (vinaigrette)
- 2 tablespoons chopped basil, dill and parsley (vinaigrette)
- Ricotta salata and prosciutto, optional

## Instructions

1. Cook the millet (1 cup dry millet to 3 cups water or light chicken stock, with a pinch of salt) and keep it warm on the side.
2. Cook the fava beans in boiling water for 1 minute. Rinse them under cold water. Remove the bean carefully from each pod; set aside.
3. Steam the peas and asparagus stems for only 1 to 2 minutes and rinse them under cold water.
4. In a small bowl, whisk the vinaigrette ingredients in this order: sea salt and pepper, mustard, lemon juice, garlic. Emulsify with the oils.
5. Top the millet with the peas, asparagus and fava beans and serve with the vinaigrette. Add slices of ricotta salata and prosciutto for a more substantial meal.
